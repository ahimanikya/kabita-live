  - text: Monalisa Dash Dwibedy is an IT Consultant by day and a poet by night. Her literary works are featured in many international journals and anthologies . She is the author of two poetry collections , Pearls of A Strawberry Moon( English) and Anjulae Smruti ( Odia) . She is a Columnist for OdishaPlus , her column “The Playful Traveller” takes the readers on a trip to the places she travels. As ardent lover of nature and mountains, she feels mountains always call her. Her goal is to befriend the Himalayan mountain ranges and talk to the moon some day.
