### Poem medium and editorial vignettes — 6 October 2026

Human Natural / Poetic Natural is medium-open: choose imagery and medium from the full poem, never assume every poem requires watercolor. The portrait treatment remains separately governed. Edition48 revision03 strengthens three poem assignments and adds three passage-specific editorial line drawings; preserve originals, stable sharing images and the editorial draft label. See `website-rulebook.md` and `../research/edition-sync/edition-48/revision-03/scope.json`.

Future edition syncs follow the same poem-first, medium-open imagery and passage-specific editorial line-art workflow, now embedded in section 3 of the [Edition Sync Playbook](../production/edition-sync/PLAYBOOK.md). One distinct illustration per poem is the default for newly synced editions; existing older editions are not retroactively changed.

### Visible reading language — 6 October 2026

Language is a primary control on edition/poem pages and in quiet reading: show Original, ଓଡ଼ିଆ, हिन्दी and English directly, outside settings. Text size and saved-reading tools remain secondary. Edition selections carry into poem links, quiet reading and available editorial variants. This supersedes older Aa-menu language placement; see `../records/visible-reading-language-2026-10-06.json`.
