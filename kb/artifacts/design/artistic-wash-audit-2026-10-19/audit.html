KBL-ART-AUD-001 · confirmed source inconsistency

Small decorative marks retain the previous palette

The waterline has fixed rust #983e30, slate #263d4b and olive #65665d. The restored inline header separator contains #963F28 and #78643A. These colours bypass the Ink & Voice mulberry/rose tokens. The content waterline is referenced by poet openings, quiet-reader titles and edition contents; utility openings suppress it intentionally. Current browser appearance has not been reverified.

Recommendation. Retain the established shapes and placement. Make decorative strokes respond to Ink & Voice light/dark colours through scoped styles or a theme-aware delivery of the existing vector. Preserve the historical SVG originals and do not recolour cover paintings, portraits or the original logo.
