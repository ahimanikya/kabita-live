Next: the reading experience

Inspect title and byline spacing, long lines, stanza breaks, enlarged text, night reading and the toolbar at 360px. Check each script with a fluent reader. Resolve the previously identified enlarged-header breakpoint concern before declaring the accessibility review complete.

Then: the shared components

Review default, hover, focus, selected, disabled, error and success states. Compare populated and empty search, long poet names, archive cards, artwork crops and missing-image treatment. Make each state clear through wording and structure as well as colour.

Credit once. Keep the voice close.

Generic generated-art captions belong in the common colophon. Meaningful artwork titles stay. Poet, translator and reviewer bylines remain beside their work. Preserve asset-specific credit requirements and attach provenance to exports that travel independently of the site.

Detailed decisions, review order and candidate spacing rules: Design system reference. The reference distinguishes implemented foundations from proposals; automated checks do not replace a complete accessibility review.

Icons: a single quiet colour.

Selected direction: Option A — Quiet ink. Use a single colour per icon, with soft terminals and familiar action shapes. The earlier laterite-accent treatment remains a study only. Individual cultural motifs and their placements continue through review.
