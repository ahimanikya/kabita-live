Current design system · 2 October 2026

A shared rhythm.
Room for each voice.

The artistic treatments developed page by page now form a shared language: paper, watercolor, a small poetic mark and a clear path to reading.

Design system by Ahimanikya Satapathy.

This chapter summarizes the applied direction. The complete design-system reference contains current rules, implementation paths and decision evidence, followed by preserved history. Older mockups and earlier chapters are context where a later rule supersedes them.

Atmosphere, then purpose.

Use the existing page watercolor, approved script families, a concise literary heading and one useful next action. Keep generous reading space while removing repeated headings and stacked gaps. Poetic invitations welcome readers; controls use plain words.

The small waterline.

Reuse the original wave-and-leaf at 115 × 23px, left aligned beneath opening copy or a poet quotation. Do not stretch it full-width. Edition contents use it after the reading actions, with only 4px below. Preserve the separate masthead ornament.

Full-width art on phones.

At 760px and below, inner opening illustrations, edition covers and poem artwork fill the content column with natural proportions and existing gutters. Portraits, icons and footer signatures keep their own sizing. Use cards for mobile poem lists and contribution tables. Compact labels never mean smaller touch targets.

A face, faithfully kept.

Use an identified photograph for each watercolor portrait. Preserve complexion, age, expression, hair, clothes and accessories; retain the original and provenance. Compare likeness before use. Missing photographs keep initials. Independent author/editor review is separate from local checks.

Reading tools that leave room to read.

Keep Aa, Share and Read quietly at the top level. Their icons use theme-aware laterite; labels remain neutral. Aa offers Original, Odia, Hindi and English; Standard, Large and Extra large. Small 12px reader labels save space, with controls at least 44px high. Popovers close on outside click, Escape or their close control. Underlining is a selection feature, without a persistent U icon.

Quiet reading removes website navigation and footer decoration. Flow poems continuously, starting another only when its title, byline, small ornament and at least three opening lines fit. Use a circular poet portrait and the colored small waterline between poems. Keep optional page-turn motion, reduced-motion support and paper sound off by default. Recited audio remains deferred.

An airy ending.

Use the artistic manuscript, stylus-and-leaf, sealed letter and organic Facebook symbols in the footer: 24px, single color, with labels on desktop and accessible icon-only links on mobile. Keep the boat in normal flow so it cannot overlap content. Shared boat size is 150 × 50px desktop / 125 × 42px mobile. The later main Archive-only single-boat treatment uses 190px / 145px; do not infer a site-wide change from that exception.

Credit once. Preserve the source.

Our Story → Credits & colophon holds design, fonts, artwork, portrait sources, hosting and generic translation disclosure. Keep poet bylines, named translators and quoted-work citations beside their work. Preserve originals, meaningful narratives and asset-specific credit requirements even where a visible caption is omitted.

Applied design is not release approval. The full reference distinguishes current rules, historical decisions, the ongoing portrait rollout and pending editorial/accessibility checks. The interactive review desk retains historical component studies; it is not a competing production specification.

Icons: a single considered colour.

Preserve the original Earth & voice geometry and one colour per symbol. Approved reading actions and selected editorial marks use theme-aware laterite; footer symbols inherit ink. The earlier multicolour studies remain historical. Reuse the four artistic footer icons rather than generic replacements.
