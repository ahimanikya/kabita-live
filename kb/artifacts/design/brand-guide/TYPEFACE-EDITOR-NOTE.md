# Editor’s note — choosing type for Kabita Live

Typography should invite the reader into a poem, then give the words room to breathe. Our English pairing balances an expressive literary voice with comfortable sustained reading.

- **Cormorant Garamond Medium (500):** English masthead, magazine masthead and titles. Its fine strokes and sculpted curves bring an intimate, bookish character to larger text. Use its true italic for large quotations; keep these generous in size.
- **Source Serif 4 Regular (400):** English poems, biographies and longer editorial passages. This quieter companion supports continued reading. Keep italics restrained and preserve every poet’s line breaks.
- **Clear interface type:** retain the current sans-serif for menus, buttons, metadata and forms. Navigation remains 17px with generous touch targets.
- **Odia:** Noto Serif Oriya Regular remains the approved family throughout; Medium is available for emphasis. The homepage signature remains dark ink, with both lines sharing a left edge.
- **Hindi — approved:** Tiro Devanagari Hindi Regular (400) for Hindi titles, poems and editorial prose. Its flowing, calligraphic character gives the Hindi pages a literary warmth. Use upright letters, natural spacing and 1.8–2 line height; do not synthesize bold or slanted text. This decision is locked alongside Noto Serif Oriya for Odia.

Select a family for the way it treats its own script. Do not force Hindi or Odia to imitate Latin serifs, add tracking inside joined letterforms, synthesize slanted Indic letters, or use ornament as a substitute for legibility. Compare matras, conjuncts, names and punctuation at actual phone size. Set script-specific sizes and line heights for comparable visual comfort rather than identical numeric values.

Before an issue goes live, verify the actual fonts loaded, inspect long titles and native names, check night reading and text enlargement, and have a fluent editor review shaping. Retain the font licenses with the publication assets.
