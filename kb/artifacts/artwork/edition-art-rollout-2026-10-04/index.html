Keeping company: stored memory, a familiar landscape becoming uncertain, and midnight shadows as companionship. Three different visual densities and media; an interpretation selected for this rollout, not a claim about the original editorial theme.

The faces we keep

The poem contrasts changing, estranged life outside with faces suspended in an album. Show the act of keeping memory without inventing the people in those photographs.

Small traces, shared life: bright festival colour, a patient creature close to the ground, and a shore after withdrawal. Vary scale, saturation and mark-making; a new interpretive direction, not a historical editorial theme.

After the shared colour

होली
Vikramaditya Singh / विक्रमादित्य सिंह

The Holi poem joins estranged people through shared celebration. Evoke the ordinary aftermath of meeting, not a staged festival advertisement or a crowd of invented faces.

Hold On
Randy Brooks

Hold On places patient small lives beside mortality. Choose the first easy-going slug among tiny flowers as one quiet encounter; leave the other haiku images to the language.

Prompt 1

After the retreat

Game of Sea
Satya Sundar Samanta

Game of Sea shifts from apparently friendly waves to what they carry away and expose. Make absence perceptible through the shore, without a literal bone or treasure tableau.
