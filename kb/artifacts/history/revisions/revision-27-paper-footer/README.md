# Revision 27 — Cotton paper, varied poem washes, one-row footer

The 37 journal pages use cotton paper except the three poem readers, which receive monsoon or earth wash through a stable hash of their poem ID. The assignment is intentionally varied but does not change on reload or rebuild. See checks.json for the mapping.

Cotton is shown at 16%; washes at 18%, fading towards plain paper down the page and softened to 12.6% on phones. Night reading, forced colours and printing hide the textures. Original texture artwork remains preserved in the background study.

The shared footer places “Poetry belongs to its respective authors.” on the left and Our story / Send a poem / Contact on the right. It uses one row on desktop, wrapping on narrow screens without shrinking type. The small boat remains; the Odia signature is not repeated.

All 41 audited journal/companion pages report zero automated axe violations and no horizontal overflow at 1280px and 320px with the mobile menu open. Pseudo-element backgrounds require separate contrast checking; revision 26 provides conservative composited-pixel checks. This is not a full accessibility certification. Previously documented 200% header enlargement issues remain outside this revision.

The visual branding guide, editor HTML/PDF reference and proposal reflect the selected direction. The PDF was rendered and visually checked. Screenshots show the actual preview.
