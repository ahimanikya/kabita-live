# A feeling of paper — background studies

Four review-only directions for Kabita Live. The live local journal and its shared stylesheet are unchanged. The study adds a temporary background style only inside its preview frame.

Open `site/background-studies/index.html` through the project’s local server. At the current preview address: http://127.0.0.1:8771/background-studies/

The board compares identical Odia/English typography, shows original textures, and offers the actual homepage, three language readers, an editor profile and archive. Controls include desktop/tablet/phone widths, strength, recommended-strength reset, plain-paper comparison and night preview on poem pages. The colour washes fade down the opening and are softened further on phones. Cotton repeats through the page. There is no animation or fixed/parallax background.

The strongest raw texture plates are shown separately to make the material differences visible. They should not be mistaken for the suggested website strength. Stronger slider settings display a reading-comfort notice.

## Readability and verification

Original PNGs were preserved and encoded as WebP for previews. At suggested strengths, conservative alpha-composited checks of the darkest pixel in each WebP against the existing ink, muted text, sea-blue and laterite text colours all exceed 4.5:1. The limiting muted-text ratios are B 4.59:1, C 4.57:1 and D 4.58:1. Detailed method and figures are in contrast.json. This evaluates colour contrast, not visual distraction or the readability of every possible page. Values above the suggested strengths are exploratory and are not claimed to pass.

Verified surface switching, strength adjustment, strong-texture notice, reset, plain-paper comparison and night-preview controls by browser interaction. Inspected the comparison grid, phone Odia opening and desktop night view. Night backgrounds are untextured; print and forced-colour rules also remove the added texture. No full site-wide accessibility certification is claimed for this exploratory study.

## Assets

Three new backgrounds were generated using the built-in image generation tool. These are creative surface studies, not scans of historical Odisha materials. Exact prompts, generated originals, saved master paths and web paths are in prompts.json. The existing fine-paper SVG remains the baseline.

No background has been selected or applied to the main journal by this revision. The enlarged-text header findings from revision 24 remain separate pending work.
