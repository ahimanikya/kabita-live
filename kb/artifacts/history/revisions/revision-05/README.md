## Cover studies

Three portrait 3:4 PNG originals were created with the built-in image-generation tool. Exact prompts and the small evening-water cleanup instruction are in `COVER-PROMPTS.txt`.

- `earth-after-rain.png`: fallen white flower on rain-darkened red earth.
- `window-and-memory.png`: clay cup and cloth beside a weathered window.
- `evening-water.png`: empty boat on blue-hour wetland water. The final image removes spurious generated marks along the bottom edge.

WebP copies in `site/assets/covers/` are format-compressed without resizing or image edits. The magazine lettering, signature and issue metadata are separate HTML text layers. The preview applies an interface gradient for legibility; it does not alter the source images.

The imagery is generated and interpretive, not documentary photography of verified locations. These are proposed studies for the cover system. Historical published covers remain preserved. Each future issue should have a distinct commissioned, licensed or appropriately labelled generated artwork chosen for its editorial purpose.
