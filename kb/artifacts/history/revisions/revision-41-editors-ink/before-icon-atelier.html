01 · Choose the character

Ink, with room for earth.

A · Quiet ink

Recommended default. A single colour and soft line endings. Closest to a small drawing in a book’s margin.

B · An earth accent

The same drawing with a pale laterite shape. Reserve for editorial introductions; keep practical controls in one colour.

04 · A small discipline

Give each mark a reason to be here.

Keep the words

Labels carry meaning. Never make a visitor guess that a conch means audio or a boat means sharing. Social platforms keep their recognisable names.

Let motifs breathe

One motif at a collection or editorial opening. Avoid decorative symbols beside every paragraph, poem or navigation item. Do not combine a motif with another divider in the same spot.

Respect the reference

These drawings are interpretations, not certified craft patterns. Pipili Chandua stays reserved. People such as Fakir Mohan belong in sourced portraits and stories, not generic interface glyphs.

Proof the small size

Editorial motifs begin at 36px. Controls use a separate 24-unit drawing at 20–24px, inside a target of at least 44px. Focus, contrast and labels remain visible.

First decision: which touch feels right?

My recommendation is Quiet ink for the main family, with earth accents only on occasional editorial motifs. Review the textile, woodland and landscape symbols before choosing which belong in the journal.
