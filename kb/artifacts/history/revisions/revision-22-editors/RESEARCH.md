# Editors · Research and implementation notes

Reviewed in the in-app browser on 29 September 2026.

## Placement and page structure

The design credit appears once, in the content of Our Story: “Reimagined and redesigned by Ahimanikya Satapathy”. All 37 journal footers retain only the authorship notice. The shared navigation now includes Editors. The dedicated landing page links to two full profiles, with portraits, approximately 230–250 words of biographical prose each, selected books, source links and links between the profiles. The earlier Pradeep Biswal archive-poem link is preserved.

## Portrait handling

Original file bytes were saved through the browser’s observed-page asset export. No generative reconstruction, retouching or identity changes were made. Layout framing is CSS only. Portrait-source captions link to the source pages. Photographer attribution and permission for public reuse were not established; the files are local design-review assets pending an editor-approved publishing copy. See portrait-provenance.json. Facebook was not needed because useful author/festival portraits were accessible.

## Source limitations

During this session the journal’s contributor-detail URLs and some PHP subpages rendered the homepage rather than distinct records. They were not treated as fresh biography or contribution evidence. The previously audited “A Poet Has No Land” archive link is retained without claiming a complete contribution history. Full verse and contributor migration remain separate work.
