# The fragrance of earth. The voice of the heart.

**ମାଟିର ମହକ। ମନର ସ୍ୱର।**

This is the feeling every page should leave behind: intimate, rooted, tender and spacious. Retain the current name, covers, palette, multilingual reading and editor cheat sheet. This note extends the existing branding guidance.

## Cover editor’s card

- Choose the issue’s emotion first: return, longing, tenderness, solitude, memory or renewal.
- Find one real, artistic image that carries it. Prefer an Odisha connection where it is meaningful: wet soil, a courtyard, a window, water and reeds, cloth, a shore, a town lane or a bookshop.
- Let Odisha appear through light, material and lived detail. Include contemporary places and people as well as landscape and craft.
- Keep one dominant subject, natural light and enough quiet space for the masthead. Preserve realistic photographic or painterly-realistic character.
- Use one distinctive image per issue. Let the restrained interiors provide continuity across changing covers.
- A photograph needs a verified place, maker and usable rights. Commissioned art names its artist and inspiration. Generated art says “Odisha-inspired artwork”; do not imply it documents a real location.
- Give culturally specific artwork to an informed regional artist, with a credit. Abstract leaf-and-water dividers are contemporary brand decoration, not claimed traditional motifs.
- Allow an image from elsewhere when it belongs more truthfully to the issue. Odisha is the journal’s grounding, not a restriction on its writers’ worlds.

## Keep the existing cover studies

**Earth after rain:** keep the fallen flower and wet-earth study. For a future original photograph, seek a real Odisha courtyard, village path or field edge after rain.

**Window and memory:** keep the clay cup and worn-window study. For a future commission, work with an actual Odisha home and natural light; acknowledge its people and place.

**Evening water:** keep the boat and wetland study. Chilika or Mangalajodi can inform a future location commission. The present generated image is not a verified photograph of either.

## Every page should carry the feeling

Home/issue: the strongest atmospheric image, then a clear invitation to read.

Poem/review: warm paper, exact language and line breaks, generous spacing and a quiet end mark. The reader should feel held by the page rather than surrounded by decoration.

Writer/editor: an approved natural portrait, careful credits, humane biography and contribution history.

Archive/directory/search: breathing room between records; the same signature, earthy ink accents and a delicate waterline under the opening heading.

Contact/feedback/error: kind, plain words, clear next steps and the same visual calm. Utility pages belong to the literary world too.

The signature appears in the shared header and footer. Keep imagery at page edges, outside the verse. Default to stillness; the optional existing homepage movement respects reduced-motion preferences. Do not scatter floating particles, artificial petals or animations through reading pages.

## Ten-second editorial check

Does the image belong to the issue? Is its Odisha association honest? Can the masthead breathe? Is the poem easy to read? Are credits accurate? Does this page feel like the same gentle literary home?
