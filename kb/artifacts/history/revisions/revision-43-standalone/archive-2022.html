Story behind the cover · Issue 2

Enough to share

The baskets hold grain, but also the thought of meals still to come. Their woven edges turn storage into a visible act of care. Beyond hunger's immediate hour lies another kind of tenderness: keeping something aside, trusting there will be people with whom to open it.

Cultural connection
Grain baskets connect woven utility, food and household memory; no specific basket tradition or community is asserted.

Story behind the cover · Issue 1

Before the first line

The cup has left a small warmth beside the open notebook. Outside, the day is already underway; on the page, nothing has yet been decided. A beginning need not arrive as a declaration. It can be this modest willingness to sit down, listen, and leave a little room for words.

Cultural connection
An imagined writer's everyday table, opening the collection through attention rather than a biographical claim about an actual poet.
