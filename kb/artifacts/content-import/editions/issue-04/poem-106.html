Poem

Amazing Evolution

Akshaya Kumar Das



ISSUE#4
February 2023

Radiant sun starts it`s day with a dawn,
With saffon radiance it`s reflections touch every one,

From living to non-living the radiance flows,
In solar radiance everyone glows,

Plants & afforetation start their osmosis process,
Prepare their food for the day in silence,

No one knows how a tree grows,
A tiny seed grows to be a tall tree in year`s,

All are creatures of mother nature,
Evolution brings every species closure,

Since infinity the relentless process of creativity,
Carrying on it`s mundanely duties with greater vision & clarity,

Nature is God`s own creation,
Provision for everyone is it`s profession & passion,

Nature treats eveyone equal,
But we segregate them to achieve our own goal,

The ultimate goal of serving & saving the humanity ,
Striking a harmony & balance between plants, animals & humans till eternity,

The planet belongs to every one,
A tiny ant to a mammoth elephant live in the domain,

Civilization has marched ahead,
With Equality in mind for everyone in head,
