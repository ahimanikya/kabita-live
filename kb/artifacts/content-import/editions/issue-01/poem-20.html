Poem

The Pain Of Unsaid Words

Diptirekha Das



ISSUE#1
November 2022

A billion stories are remain untold
And a million voices are going unheard.
Each passing day
We are racing fast against the time
When our folks needed our time
To sit by them and talk for a while
We were always running out of time.
By the time we realise
We should have listen to them for a while;
They are now no longer around.
Perhaps they exist beyond this mortal world’s physical entity.
It hurts
Because something left unspoken.
And that never going to be heard anymore.
Nothing hurts that much like your unsaid words.
In spite of your thousands trying when you are unable to tell those words.
That remains within you and left unshared.
It pains
When your story remains untold.
Even with an ocean of relations when you’re badly struggling
To find that someone to share your emotions.
The Pain of unsaid words are immeasurable.
Then why not building up a strong hold with the possession you have now ?
You never know
Spending times listening to their sides
May save you from that lifelong mourning
That you’re not there at the time of need to listen.

Author: Diptirekha Das

Diptirekha Das is a bilingual poet;basically writing in English and Odia. She is also a blogger blogging both in English & Hindi. She hails from Bhubaneswar, Odisha. She is a post graduate in Economics from Utkal University. Literature is her passion. And writing poetry is her most doing activity so far. She has authored two poetry books in English & one book in Odia. She has also co-edited a multilingual literature anthology. Her poems, blogs & articles were featured in various national & international literary magazines. She loves to write addressing contemporary issues regarding women &society as well.
