Poem

While searching for the peace………….

Subash Singh Parajuli



ISSUE#31
May 2025

While searching for the peace
A body stitched with plastic rupees
The aim of life is robbed
Unfathomable moment of joy dropped
An art of living dare to stink
A fasting soul started to drink
A cup of cold blood as honey
All the rituals and rites sold with money

While searching for peace in alcoholism
A couple of toxicants atoned metabolism
A folk flag swims in poison
The fertility of the mind is in the process of erosion
Nothing is in time and ritual
For the adoration of a grave that has no transparent visual

While searching for the peace in religion
A holy temple imbued
A saint of the church seduced
A tranquil idol of Buddha reduced
A shrine of a mosque fused
When the light of men wipe-out
By the memory of the little many-frame brain
The fresh volume of memoir is in the gap of salvation
A howl of rage and love are in dreary oblivion

While searching for the peace in ritual
The delightful blessing of God missed by the priest
Who rode on nuns like the beast
Mokshya is climbing up Sinner Hill
Sins of gutter tempted oneself to kill
While searching for the peace through luxury
His rotted body arrived at the palace
Whose empathy and sympathy prolonged in somebody`s shoe lace
And his soul hurling to swing through ripped heart
Whose roots lie no longer but apart

While searching for the peace in humanity
A man is a cannibal
His descendants are animal
God be serf
Serf is god`s dwarf
While searching for peace through good relation
Father has kissed daughter`s breast
A servant puts stink maiden in his hit least
Whiz kids sing and laugh with opium fete
And cheers as urging men to live or die as their wish before departing from heaven`s gate

While searching for the peace in science
Moon crumbled for future shelter
Universe captured as for theatre of robotic act
The sea will have a jug of filthy water
For bathing mask-and costume robot

While searching for peace in emotion
Many intimacies burn with a heap of anger
Loads of desires provoke to lead with a single finger
Usual theory and principles drag to link with murder
And a half-size brain fools billions with its seductive order

While searching for the peace in beauty
A half span of life swings behind the curtain
Half-life line is limping for birth`s pertinent
A string of peace jingles within a wink of the eye
Obscurity of life foams through the auction of cry.
