# Five-poem translation pilot

Proposals for discussion; no live text changed. Full sources and current versions were rechecked against saved fingerprints. Each target was compared directly with its source. These are same-assistant drafts, not fluent-reader or author approval.

## I Will Return · 777

Prabhanjan K. Mishra · Issue 45

Keep the weight of the promise without an inaccurate word count.

### Original — I WILL RETURN

```text
A seed of hope
a committed trust
like the words
of Casabianca`s father
that made the boy defy
the burning deck.

Myth is the witness
in man`s court,
when the naughty boy
left his fickle girl,
the separation suddenly assumed
a cosmic seriousness -

"I will return" the three magic words
of the cow herd weighed heavier
than the other magical utterances -
"I love you" etc. Suddenly
years piled up over the urchin`s age,
she riping into instant womanhood.

Today, in temple`s sanctum
Radha is made to stand
by Krishna, but she
cringes away in disgust,
"He is Rukmini`s husband,
not my Kanha."

But unlike abducted Puro of Pinjar
across the border,
who shouted back, "I will return".
Her people heard and rejoiced
but her promise ordained
an unrequited nationhood.

It kept hanging across
the cursed border - stitching
love, loss with threads of tears.
Life oscillating between
her past and present
she compromised with an inevitabilty.

Unlike the rain that returns
every monsoon, pouring its promise
into dreary lips of cracked mud.
A sigh of petrichor blows
to assuage the asphyxiated dry seed
down the earth`s womb.
```

Current:

```text

घृणा से पीछे हटती है,

उसके लोगों ने सुना और हर्षित हुए,

लटका रहा—आँसुओं के धागों से
प्रेम और खोना सीता हुआ।
जीवन झूलता रहा
उसके अतीत और वर्तमान के बीच,

उस बारिश से भिन्न, जो लौटती है
हर मानसून, अपना वादा उँडेलती हुई
फटी मिट्टी के उदास होंठों में।
सोंधी गंध का एक निःश्वास बहता है,
दम घुटते सूखे बीज को ढाढ़स देने,
धरती के गर्भ में।
```
