Heart, the impervious cave..

By Manorama Choudhury

Reader tools

Taller the building Deeper its foundation Prettier the outer layer Muddier the root structure Wider the building’s perimeter Complex the root architecture As life inches towards its afternoon Dreams and desires Scattered and sacrificed Deduced to thin air Even after, the heart that holds Respect and love for others Sparkles like gold Though burnt with fire. Just like the building Gives shelter to many And stands tall The man who supports others Despite his pain, he smiles And spreads love and affection To one and all He turns to an ever-flowing river, Withstands the test of time after all. Though gives a shelter to others Like a wandering bird He finds a little space in the impervious cave Of his own heart Just for himself..
