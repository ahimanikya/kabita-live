Daughter

By Alok Kumar Ray

After the aroma of a rapport
That is not being shaken
That is beyond the clutches of hugging
That is afar the capacity of an  eye to grab
Spreads anonymous wings,
With the backing of a conspirator cloud, the rain
Clapping its hands created a fake river.
Drawing the line of  partition while the river was flowing
Water bubbles burst with laughter.
Waves in the form of braids
Stretched from this bank to that bank.
Instead of causing landslips
They turn into an overpass that touched both banks.
Getting the ford, getting the shelter, bonding grew up.
After the wrath of the unseasonable rain caved in
After the vanishing of the bigoted river
The intricacy of rowing the boat
Since long is being mortgaged sans notice.
There is no dispute that could insist overpowering
Not a viable trace is visible now.
The flat chest of that dead river now adorns
Flowers and fruits  aplenty, fortune and affection
Where sings the song of delight and contentment,
The time of a daughter, defiant and wild.
