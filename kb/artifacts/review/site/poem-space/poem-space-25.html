Realization

By Nandita Samanta

Reader tools

I realised we grow more out of the pain.
Losing makes thinking more mature,
and us more sensible.
We eventually get through the loss,
tossing time aside we collapse into the pinhead of an end.
Again reset to zero.
Ahead we go.
A feeling slowly fades, and time slowly heals
sweating through the summers,
getting into the warm covers in winter,
we cover a distance unknowingly
-between moments: singular, collective, ten years, a year ago, yesterday.
We melt so quickly into the present
that one day a memory becomes a story.
We outgrow everything until the last day,
accommodating to any situation,
not ready to accept any stagnation or stuntedness.
