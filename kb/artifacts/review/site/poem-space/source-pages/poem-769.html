Invitation

By Antara Mukherjee

Reader tools

Torrential rain enlightens me about
the bygone of care. Bareness, punctuated
with fine, black threads, weave a design of desire.
Silver rays of golden moon caress the ascetic
and wake him from a slumber of aridity.

Exhausted from the onslaught of time,
his in-built curves could retain the slippery hedge,
where, once, neon lights shone through smoky days,
and, pleasure, splashed from the mug, transformed into
sonorous sounds of satisfaction.

Enroute the gorge, a magnet-like moment
stops at the globes; a pinkish brown terrain unveils
to shame the moist earth. Before I slip into a dream,
ambitious eyes – sleepy yet sharp – invite me
to a destination, unknown.
