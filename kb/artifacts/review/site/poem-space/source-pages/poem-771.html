Absence

By Lopamudra Banerjee

Old memories masquerade, dream-like.
They write themselves
against the scarred flesh and muscle of Time.
Was it a tinge of taunting friendship
or a wild rendezvous of a short-lived,
whirlwind romance?
The molten grief of dead ones
who dwindled, by and by, like fallen leaves?
Forgotten lyrics and rhythms of a dying heart
that had once, throbbed with a symphony of fire?
In this dusty landscape now, my hissing prose
my rattling rhyme sings of absence,
the absence of the luminous wings,
the precarious secrets churning,
the rain of migrant music that welled as tears
the clamor of voices that tangled
the chilly evening air.
In absence, I find the note, the sound, the want
traversing through my every molecule.
I ingest the melting soul of the missing puzzles
and the music of nothingness bubbles up.
The nothingness that makes the flesh and bones,
the sinew and muscles of my nameless verses.
