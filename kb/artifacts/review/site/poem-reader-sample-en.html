Drink

By Majrooh Rashid

Every evening
late into the night
I drink life
draught by draught
and its bitterness runs
through all my veins
till emptiness
takes over everything
including my sleep.
Kafka emerges from the shadows
dancing on the wall in the light
of the wavering lamp
and Camus within analyzes
nothingness
pouring out from my restless pores
all night.
At daybreak I am convinced
that nothingness
can be converted into thingness
though it needs a lot of love
for undeterred pain and resolute anguish.

Majrooh Rashid
