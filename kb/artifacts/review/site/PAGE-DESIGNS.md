## Shared identity

**କବିତା ଲାଇଭ. / Kabita Live** retains the established name. **ମାଟିର ମହକ। ହୃଦୟର ସ୍ବର।** appears in the header and footer on every journal template. The existing English epigraph remains a separate literary invitation. Warm paper, red earth, script-specific reading fonts and watercolor at the page edges connect the pages. Cover studies use realistic individual imagery, while verse sits on quiet paper.

The masthead is Odia-led with a clearly readable Roman companion. Odia, Hindi and English reading choices have equal access. Language filtering never implies that a translation exists. Fonts load from Google in this preview; production should use properly licensed self-hosted files.

## Source coverage

The public archive supplies 47 issue records and the contributor directory supplies 427 visible name/profile records. All 16 current poem titles and credits were checked on their detail pages. The eight listed book reviews and four highlights are indexed. Metadata snapshots are in `revision-06/` in the complete package.

Three local poem readers contain short attributed excerpts, not complete poems. Three local writer profiles list 4, 23 and 4 sourced contributions; older works open their original published pages. Other indexed poems, writers and older issues retain explicitly marked original links. Editor Pradeep Biswal has one verified contribution; the managing editor's old link returned to the homepage, so a biography was not invented.

See `revision-06/AUDIT-AND-HANDOFF.md` for the complete route/source map, findings, source URLs and production sequence. Admin/unpublished pages and database completeness were outside the public audit.

## Working interactions

- Current-poem Unicode title/byline search, language filters and no-results state.
- Complete writer name search plus A–Z filtering; names and legacy IDs retained.
- Full archive year filtering, year collections and recent cover/list views.
- Writer contribution title and year filtering.
- Reading sizes, night mode, correction context and mobile navigation.
- Accessible native share dialog with original URL, title and author; copy link/caption, device share when available, WhatsApp and Facebook destinations. External posting remains the reader's action.
- Response/contact validation and local preview confirmation. No messages are sent or stored; email links open a draft.

The share-card preview is not a downloadable image or crawler-visible Open Graph implementation. Reading preferences currently last only for the open page. Full-text search and migration remain production work.

## Artwork and production limits

The three generated photographic covers are proposed studies, not historical published covers. Original image files and exact prompts are in `revision-05/`. The earlier watercolor is atmosphere inspired by Odisha, not claimed to be an authenticated traditional artwork or documentary place record. Approved portraits remain to be supplied.

Full content migration, real moderation, final copy/rights review, CMS publishing, redirects and actual-device accessibility checks remain in the handoff. `VERIFICATION.md` records what was checked. No live website changes have been made.
