Kabita Live · A cover with something to say

ମାଟିର ମହକ · ମନର ସ୍ୱର

1. Choose one anchor: a place, living craft, everyday object, ecological detail or literary person.

2. Name one feeling: anticipation, care, witness, play, rest, loss or belonging.

3. Write a two-sentence premise before commissioning the artwork. Keep one focal idea.

4. Compare the previous six editions. Avoid the same object and framing; vary the emotional note and balance of ivory, laterite, sea and hearth. The current 47-cover collection has intentional family resemblance, not 47 unrelated styles.

5. Write an original 40–70 word story. Keep a separate factual cultural note and link the source for named heritage. Do not manufacture a quotation or infer original issue contents from a new cover.

6. Use the English Kabita Live masthead, the Odia signature and verified issue/date as separately typeset elements. Preserve original artwork and source references.

7. Check likeness for named personalities, textile structure for crafts, and geography for named places. Mark AI artwork as interpretive; do not call it a documentary photograph.

8. Place the story in an optional disclosure. Keep imagery outside verse and maintain readable menus and touch targets.

Voices to return to

- Fakir Mohan Senapati: attention to ordinary people, language and social observation. The current portrait is a generated interpretive tribute, not a historical photograph. Government context: https://odisha.gov.in/en/odisha-profile/eminent-personalities/fakir-mohan-senapati

- Gangadhar Meher: rhythm, nature and a weaver-family connection. Current loom story is imaginative, not a claim that it depicts his belongings. Source: https://sambalpur.odisha.gov.in/en/about-district/eminent-personalities

- Kuntala Kumari Sabat: a future literary-voice study, anchored in a selected, verified work. Confirm biography and likeness before portrait commissioning. Reference: https://magazines.odisha.gov.in/orissaannualreference/ORA-2004/pdf/eminent_literacy_luminaries_of_orissa.pdf

- Gopabandhu Das: a future poetry-and-service study, beginning with a verified poem and memorial material. Reference: https://odishatourism.gov.in/content/tourism/en/discover/attractions/museums/gopabandhu-das-memorial-museum.html

These are editorial directions, not claims that the journal has published or commemorated these figures in specific issues. Future portraits are not generated in this revision.
