Life and writingSelected books

A storyteller’s way of seeing.

Paresh Kumar Pattnaik writes fiction in Odia. Literary-festival and publisher profiles also use the spelling Paresh Kumar Patnaik. His work moves between the short story, the novel and writing for younger readers.

Puri Literature Festival’s profile describes a storyteller who approaches social questions through humour and compassion. It highlights his questioning of superstition and religious fanaticism, and records an Odisha Sahitya Akademi award for his work as a novelist. This is a literary practice concerned with how people live together, as well as how a story is told.

Many forms of a story.

His novels include Shoka Divas, Katha Thila and Seshara Arambha. Among his short-story collections are Iswar Aniswar, Abasistansha, Bhasi Jaa Mora Kagaj Danga, Swan Brutanta and Hrudayahin. The festival profile also notes that his stories have reached readers through translations into English, Hindi and other Indian languages.

Room for the younger reader.

Shalandi’s author catalogue makes another part of his range visible: children’s and young readers’ books alongside fiction for adults. It lists Musa O Musi, Goindagiri, Chor Police, Chori Bahir Rahasya, Nadiabari Bhut and Bhutar Bhet, as well as titles such as School re Chori and Nila Shirt Ra Rahasya.

For a reader meeting his work for the first time, this range offers several starting points: a novel, a collection of short stories or a book to share with a younger reader. The selected titles below link to the publisher’s listings, while the festival biography offers a wider introduction to his literary work.
