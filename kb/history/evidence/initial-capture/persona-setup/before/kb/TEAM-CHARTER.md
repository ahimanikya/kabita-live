# Kabita Live · redesign charter

Purpose: make a long-running multilingual poetry magazine feel literary, welcoming, grounded in Odisha and simple to read. Preserve its existing Kabita Live identity and respect the publication’s poets, editors, history and original materials.

Audience: readers of Odia, Hindi and English; contributing writers; magazine editors; Odisha residents and diaspora.

Accountable human for this redesign workspace: **Ahimanikya Satapathy**. He directs this project and approves design choices. This does not claim he founded, owns or edits the existing magazine. The publication’s editorial and content rights remain with their actual holders.

Scope: research, design system, local prototype, original icon candidates, artwork stewardship, accessibility checks and reviewable handoff. Existing authorization covers routine preparation and verification within the brief. Public deployment, new spending, outside commitments and changes to magazine governance are not part of this conversion.

Success: readable three-script typography; consistent pages; clear writer/issue journeys; 47 distinct cover narratives; accessible controls; a useful editor guide; source-linked records; explicit distinction between candidates and approved work.

No human reports to AI. The current assistant supports Ahimanikya within this chat’s scope. Blueprint persona names do not appoint or activate anyone here.

Adoption authority and scope: [actual direction](records/adoption-decision.json). Durable work status: [dashboard](registers/DASHBOARD.md).
