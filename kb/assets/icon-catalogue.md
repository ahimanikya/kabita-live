## Reuse rules

- 14 editorial motifs: 48-unit masters, use at 36–72px. 21 utility symbols: 24-unit masters, use at 20–24px in targets of at least 44px. Approved footer icons use24px and1.35px strokes.
- Use one colour per icon. Footer symbols inherit ink; later approved reading actions and selected editorial marks use theme-aware laterite. Multicolour studies remain historical. Keep labels beside unfamiliar motifs and controls.
- Preserve the existing logo and header separator. No decorative marks beside every paragraph or verse.
- Cultural references are interpretations, not certified weave patterns, ritual diagrams or botanical/topographic evidence.
- Keep Pipili Chandua reserved. Famous writers belong in sourced portraits and biographies, not generic action glyphs.
- The public SVG sprite and manifest live under site/assets/icons/earth-voice-v1. Original geometry was drawn for Kabita Live; no third-party icon paths were copied.
- No shared external icon repository has been connected. The project catalogue is the starting library the user requested.
