# Literary website benchmark

Qualitative research on six established literary destinations, not an independently verified traffic ranking. Full source-linked comparison is the local site page `literary-study.html`.

| Reference | Observed strength | Candidate lesson for Kabita Live |
|---|---|---|
| [Poetry Foundation](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/topics-themes) | Topic/theme discovery | A small set of curated themes near poem results |
| [Poets.org](https://poets.org/poems) | Title/text/poet search, themes, forms and occasions | First-line search and a recurring reading invitation |
| [Rekhta](https://www.rekhta.org/) | Poet/poem/tag links, script renderings and sharing | Local script choices and attributed quote sharing |
| [The Paris Review](https://www.theparisreview.org/poetry) | Name/decade archive and issue identity | Clear writer → poem → issue navigation |
| [Asymptote](https://www.asymptotejournal.com/) | Visible translators/source languages and issue art | Preserve translator bylines; link each cover story |
| [Poetry Archive](https://poetryarchive.org/) | Recordings, poets and collections | “Listen” only when a sourced recording exists |

Recommendations preserve the locked typography, palette, main navigation and footer. They do not introduce compulsory accounts, advertising, carousels, automated translations or autoplay.

Inspection: direct visual browser review of Poets.org, Rekhta, The Paris Review and Asymptote; official text for Poetry Archive; indexed official Poetry Foundation pages after direct fetch failure. No comparative traffic dataset or complete competitor accessibility audit was obtained.

Utkal Blueprint is the adopted record/decision framework. The separate Utkal Project brand study influenced coordinated optical sizes and variant management; its book-and-boat symbol and exact palette were not copied.
