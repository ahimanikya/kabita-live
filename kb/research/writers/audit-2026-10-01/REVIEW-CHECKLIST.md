## How to use the flags

Do not remove every flagged paragraph automatically. Resolve a question using an identity-matched primary source, or soften/attribute the claim; omit it if useful support cannot be established. Record the evidence and decision before closing an item. Preserve captured originals and existing translator credits. No writer has received independent factual clearance from this audit.

The automatically generated poem commentary is **literary interpretation**, not author-approved biography. Keep a short, clearly scoped reading of the poems; avoid treating poetic speakers as autobiographical or extrapolating a whole career from one poem. Consider shortening these passages during our page review. This audit did not re-read every poem.
