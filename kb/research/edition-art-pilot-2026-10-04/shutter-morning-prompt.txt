Poem read as context (do not render this text):
Towards spring`s end
another day is just starting.
I hear motorbike horns
shivering engines of old public buses
and voices from the local teashops
amid cycle bells
and squeaks of rolling shutters.
The day is getting off to a typical start.
It will follow the sun
with the same shadows as yesterday.
As I head farther away from the night
I must say goodbye to you.
Such is the nature of daylight.
We sink in it, see and think,
think more and move apart.
We begin to peep and grieve
and time passes over fields
taking away the shadows
of storm-beaten trees.
