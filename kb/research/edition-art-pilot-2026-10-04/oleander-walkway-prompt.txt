This morning
I watch
cones of oleander

The oleanders open
their yellow
funnel

The funnel invites
honeybees
in clusters

In clusters they
enter the frills
of the flowers

The flowers
pour their honey
on the walkways

On the walkways
the sun draws
an invisible line

An invisible line
I listen to the music
float by
