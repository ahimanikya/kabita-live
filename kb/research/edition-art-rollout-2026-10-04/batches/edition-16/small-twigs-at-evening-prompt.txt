Poem Twigs of hope by Sujata Dash; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
As clouds of fluster
shroud my life
I pinch me a little
to test whether I am alive

writhing under
sobs and whimpers
hurt pride and vexation mingle
and my modest soul
slowly gets invaded by delirium

seasons sweep down
the aisle of time
and I get mauled by
mounds of grief
sniffle with discomfiture
the impasse does not end
nor the harsh blows soften

my ardent wishes curl up
too tightly, repel to unfurl
desires too swallow bitter pills
slowly beaten into submission
In trying to squelch
the rage and rising fury within
twigs of hope I pick
to festoon my tender evening.
