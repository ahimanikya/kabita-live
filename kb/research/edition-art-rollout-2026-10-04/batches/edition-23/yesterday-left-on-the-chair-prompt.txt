Poem Timeless Echoes by Rashmi Mohapatra/ ରଶ୍ମି ମହାପାତ୍ର; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
Words, heavy with
yesterdays,
hang like
forgotten cloaks.

I rise to greet now
with arms wide,
catching tomorrow
like constellations
in a velvet sea.

Dreams,
wrapped in hope,
fly true,
homing like swallows.

I stand, resolute,
a beacon
in the tempest of time,
mapping stars
of my own design.

Stride by stride,
a doorway opens,
inhale by inhale,
a new sunrise.

Anchored
in the present,
I sculpt the future
with whispers
of the past.
