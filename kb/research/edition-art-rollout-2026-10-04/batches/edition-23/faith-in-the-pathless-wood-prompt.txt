Poem Keshav, I am Lost by Bina Singh; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
Keshav, I am lost
Drifting helplessly
Losing my way to home
The woods are deep
The darkness thickening
Crawling in the darkness
Desperately look for your Light
Where are You?
Why have You disowned me?
Am I not worthy of Your love?
Splinters of wood, caught my eyes
I fail to see the path
Stuck in the pathless wood
Yearn for my Krishna, my Saviour
Pleading rescue, from the haunting darkness
My heart craves for Your Bliss
Darkness has stretched everywhere
Leaving no space for Light
I fear this endless gloom
Road to Liberation is blocked
Huge impediments, stand between You and me
Like the great wall of China
How shall I reach You?
Floating in the sea of uncertainty
I still cling to my unbeaten faith
Holding It amid the absolute darkness
My dauntless faith, continues to pray
That, Your Light will ultimately conquer
