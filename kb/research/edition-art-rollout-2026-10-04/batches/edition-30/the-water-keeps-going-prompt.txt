Poem THE WATERFALL by Abeera Mirza; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
They say water symbolizes the flow of life,
But for me, this form of water symbolizes my dream;
Like this waterfall my dream will keep flowing in my veins,
I won`t stop until the day comes that I achieve my dream.

Even if it is far and difficult to reach,
I will do everything to succeed;
I know the journey will not be easy,
Many hurdles and barriers along the way.

The path is rocky and steep,
Lots of grass and wild animals around;
But no matter how dangerous the sorroundings,
And I may fall or stumble many times,
I will repeatedly get up and stand.

Again and again, I will continue to climb until I reach the top.
Whether sweaty and tired,
I will watch with a smile in the beautiful atmosphere,
As I savor the sweetness of success.
