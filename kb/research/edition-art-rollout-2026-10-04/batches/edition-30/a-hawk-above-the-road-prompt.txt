Poem HAWKS by Sheila Mary Fraser Porter; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
I saw five today
Sitting atop lamp posts
Waiting to swoop down and grab their prey
Be they moles, mice, rats, snakes, squirrels and even songbirds
A tasty morsel for lunch

Hawks are revered for their keen sense of sight and ability to
fly at great heights
Seeing a hawk denotes guardianship and power
They are known as the protectors and messengers of the air

Hawks display aggression by holding their body level to the ground
Drooping their wings
Raising the feathers on their heads, neck and back
They are agile flyers, that when hunting, may also take to the
ground, running and hopping to seize their prey

I also saw fifteen or twenty ducks enjoying a grassy lunch
on a farmer`s field
Having a gab session with their playmates
And the same lone duck I see every week was going for a swim
in very shallow water

They are all magnificent creatures in their own unique way
Created by the Master of the Universe!
