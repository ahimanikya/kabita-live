Poem Life Without You by Reetwika Banerjee; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
The turquoise waves
Brushed me again
With a thousand kiss!
On a sunbathed morning
You make me feel your silent touch
From the sunken depth of time.
You rest in peace
Deep within me
Like the deep blue sea...
As the briny winds embrace me
I can feel your tender love
Waiting alone by the shore.
With hands stretched at you
My heart yearns for you
Why do you go back?
The world sees a smiling face
But only the golden sands
Know the bleeding me
Torn apart in your grief.
I come back with an injured soul
And empty hands.
Everytime!
