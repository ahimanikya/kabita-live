Poem The Ancient Temple by Satyananda Sarangi; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
These ancient pillars as remnants of time
Stand mute and gaze at the fallen mankind,
All aware of rising and tumbling men;
They have left many a legend behind,
Written with sorrow and blood and valour.
Bring me those voices lost in mystery
And plaintive songs they carry beneath them,
With fine lyrics older than history.
