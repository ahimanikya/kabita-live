Poem An Ode to Kashi by Sakti Sekhar Dash; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
With the gentle murmurs of the Ganga
Kashi sleeps dreaming.
What are but those dreams?
Nameless passions, countless desires
Hopes beyond count, burning in the fire.
The wind sifts through the smoking pyre
Lulling unquiet souls to sleep,
Caressed by the waves of the Ganga
Floating into oblivion.
Is it life? Is it death?
The temple bells ring
Sending countless feet hurrying
To witness the flames circling.
Heads bow in devotion
Lips muttering silent prayers
And the heart`s fervent desires.
Sorrowful hands take the torch
Setting fire to a million dreams,
Hopes and desires beyond count
That indifferent feet trample
Beneath piles of cold ashes.
Kashi comes to life
With a thousand blended notes
Of prayer bells and conch shells
Beckoning the faithful
And the solemn funeral chants
Conveying the dead
To the crackling pyres.
Haggling hawkers and boatmen call
The pious offering prayers
A price for a lifetime of piety
Holy water of the Ganga
Enclosed in a bottle,
But a thousand blended notes drown
In the murmur of the Ganga.
