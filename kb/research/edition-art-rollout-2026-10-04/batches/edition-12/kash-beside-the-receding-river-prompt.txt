Poem There I Live by Sangarm Jena; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
I live in a remote village
where sun rises
behind the distant hills
where evening melts into night
under the dusts of cows’ feet
and children return from school
drenching in mid-September rain,
where night deepens
with the whispers of ghosts
and laughter of fairies
at the bathing ghat,
moonlight spread over
paddy fields and
wind sings with the swaying
of Kash flowers
on the banks of half-dried river.
Here I move
begging for your love
in my isolation and absences.
I live in the whispers of shadows
where our forgotten wishes
close their doors indefinitely
and play your familiar game
that I watch without a sound.
I live in a place
far away from our touch.
