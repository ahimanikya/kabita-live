Poem BE THE POEM by Prahallad Satapathy/ଡ଼.ପ୍ରହଲ୍ଲାଦ ଶତପଥୀ; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
Writing poem is not a home task
And one is not duty bound to write
The sky is a poem
The sea is a poem
The poet is an invisible entity
Who writes a poem with out writing
The way sprouts the blade of grass
The way flows the murmuring brook .

Erect a mature word and name It mountain
Poetry a lifesize image of thing
Wait for the opportune moments for the poetry
to come to heart`s deserted street .

Poetry graze all the grasses and weeds
Make sure,ears are alive to bleating
Don`t get disturbed
In poetry frogs do croak when the sky is overcast with rain
Poetry is a stream of consciousness that flows like words,water, paper boats
And swim like fishes .

Poetry is water deep
Poetry is word deep
Poetry is meditation deep
Body mind and soul becomes one.

Just go away from yourself at least for a while
Stroll, talk to tree, makes leaves your feathery wings and fly
The sky is the limit .

Beehives and ants creates poetry
Not the gigantic trees,skyscraper buildings
Poetry is always with the children of the lesser God .

Open your eyes the way the flower opens its Petals
Talk to a new born kid
Talk to an aged sick tree
Talk to a dead body
Be the ocean yourself
Create your own tidal waves
Make your own shores
Create temple of sands and destroy
Make your own traces through your feet and erase .

Find a poem every now and then
Write a poem every now and then
And if you fail to find
Be the poem yourself.
