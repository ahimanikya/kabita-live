Poem SHORING by Prabhanjan K. Mishra; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
Sense and sensibility
peace and rest
adrift floatsam and jetsam

They don`t weep
sigh or sob in silence
gone dry and gritty

I lose things, my glasses,
mobile, watch and pen
I find a few, others yet at large

I look for your eyes, breaths,
susurration of your sari rubbing my flank
the space lies like an empty morgue

The dying moon at small hours
of the night, the wind`s cautious steps
seem pretences and proxies

My damned and cursed silences
sleeplessness and heart`s missed beats
go chasing you along your shoreline

Coasting to a safe harbour
you have built there are my broken
bits and ends of crumbling ego

They try bringing you blue feathers
quiet ripples and yellow petals
and the last line of a poem of ours

You to add `The end` or your own
lines of smile or sulk, and my wishes
can`t be horses; I reduced to a begger.
