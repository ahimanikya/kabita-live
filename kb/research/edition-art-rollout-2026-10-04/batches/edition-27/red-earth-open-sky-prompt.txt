Poem Waiting For The Red Soil On My Skin by Aneek Chatterjee; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
I will take a rebirth with
green valleys on my eyes;
red soil on my skin
and black magic on my hair,
not inside my brain.

I will not take any rebirth inside
what you call society; inside what
you call universities; or on the upper
pedestal from where people look
vague and hazy and tiny.
In this birth, I have laws peeping into
and smashing
my bones; education to make me
distant and laughable; manners to
separate me
from red soils, green valleys.
In my next birth, laws will flow
like water to the thirsty mouth;
wretched soil.
Education will be only trees,
branching out to the sky;
Universities will be oceans; humans
will be animals of the
close-knit herd.
Waiting for the smell of new soil;
for the smile of new skins and bones;
for laws to illuminate a new mind,
a new, cherished magic.
