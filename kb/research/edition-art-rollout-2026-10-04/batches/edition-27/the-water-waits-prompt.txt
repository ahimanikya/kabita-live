Poem The lovelorn crane by Paramita Mukherjee Mullick; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
Every winter the last of the Siberian cranes keeps returning to Iran.
To search for his beloved Arezoo, who is no more.
Every winter Omid the crane flies back 6000km to meet his mate.
Maybe he still believes that his dear one is waiting for him.

Cranes unlike us civilized humans, mate for life.
They are monogamous and pair with their partners lifelong.
Omid and his mate Arezoo used to migrate together in the winters.
They danced together and expressed love with gentle and resonant voices.

Alas! Fifteen years ago the lovelorn cranes were separated as Arezoo died.
But Omid has been flying to Iran every winter.
Maybe like his name he still hopes that he will meet his beloved.
Every winter, he wishes he will meet his mate.

But this year he was not seen by the birders.
The last Siberian crane did not migrate this winter.
Where is he, where is the lovelorn crane?
Is he alive or has he at last met his beloved?
