Poem Moon by Devika Tripathy; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
The silver crust of teardrop
embellished with gray flowers
And bunny ears
Brings solace to my melancholy
For eons, for eternity.
The hallowed mate
To my solitary hymns
The tranquilizing shawl of dews
On the dying embers of my soul..
Moon.
In its myriad forms,
It`s the trusted keeper,
The confidant,
of my unuttered thoughts
And the melting oceans
beneath my eyes.
The witness of silent wincings
And scintillating jingles
of memories,
Moon.
The morsel of nectar
To my ravenously yearning soul,
The lunar friend of mine
Who chooses to stay
Sharing my vigil through
The blur of night,
Gazing by the window forevermore,
Moon.
Look up westward,
Into the horizon!
My Moon has returned to join me..
Like always.
