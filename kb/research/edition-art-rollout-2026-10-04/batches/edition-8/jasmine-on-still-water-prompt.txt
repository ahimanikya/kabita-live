Flower petals, long forgotten, between pages old
The withered face brightened up, dubious memory unsold
Paise five, a lozenge bribe, conscripted in the battle royal
Sister;s love, a high ransom, runs the child fast
The irresponsible words, the letters, the yellow ashes of teenager;s fervour
