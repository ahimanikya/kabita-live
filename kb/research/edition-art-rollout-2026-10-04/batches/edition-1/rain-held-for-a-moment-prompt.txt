Full poem context only; never render words: It rains ... and rains by Tejaswini Patil
Tapping on the inner plane of silence.
I try to pin every drop
With tiny words.
Some are trapped,
Some escape from the grip.
They create kaleidoscopic patterns
To be lost into.
The rain, on greens,
Refreshing the earth around
The rain, on petals,
Transcending the beauty in surround.
The rain, on cheeks
Mixed with rain within unbound,
The rain, on the umbrella
Soaking the eyes spellbound.
The rain, in dreams
To be lost and found.
Originating from your land,
It excavates my existence
From the Present;
And rehabilitates in the previous incarnation.
