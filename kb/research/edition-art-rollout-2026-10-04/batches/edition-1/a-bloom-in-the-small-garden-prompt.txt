Full poem context only; never render words: Two Poems by Sujata Parashar
Paper Flowers
Isolated and holed indoors
I took to spending most of my time
on my terrace garden tending to
potted plants of different shapes and sizes
meant to dress up the limited space.
Swishing and swooshing with the autumn wind
they continued shedding their leaves
Winter was approaching
And then one day, it caught my eye
a little bloom on the vine
joyfully dangling enjoying the sun
celebrating life
I recalled my mentor’s words;
‘winter always turns to spring.’

Scrambled Eggs
Gently stirred, whipped and beaten
My mom is an expert in egging the egg
with a pinch of salt and pepper
a dollop of butter and some fresh herbs.
She’s a scrambler
knows how to quickly
make the most of everything
even if it’s just an egg.
