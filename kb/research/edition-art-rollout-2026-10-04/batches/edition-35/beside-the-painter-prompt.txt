Poem The Ego Maniac and the Empath by Santosh Bakya; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
Grandiose narcissists
devoid of empathy and compassion
troop in and out of the world stage
creating a nauseating din; grinning.
Why do they love themselves so much -
these ego maniacs,
manipulating everyone to their selfish ends?

Bending backwards,
forwards,
sideways,
left,
right,
right,
left,
always!

Has a whimsical painter gone haywire, painting harum-scarum?
humming some grotesque tune, sitting on the pavement, crooning?

The egomaniacs wonder,
looking condescendingly at the singer-painter,
crinkling their noses, striking belligerent poses,
trying to downplay the artist.

" That splash of paint looks a little out of place,
And your pitch is too high ."
One says arrogantly, a grimace on his face,
kicking a skeletal dog

But the empathetic singer-painter continues singing  and painting,
ignoring the egomaniac`s grandiose sense of self-conceit.
He is not accountable to anyone.

He has again started dabbing.
Splashing, singing - doing his own thing.
The resentful narcissist beats a hasty retreat,
mumbling imprecations.

The mongrel whimpers, nestling next to the painter.
The painter smiles, patting him on the head.
splashing vibrant hues in his painting.
Life is, after all, a celebration. Is it not?
