Poem PEARLDOM by Mandakini Bhattacharjee; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
I bought them cheap
on a whimsy.
Testing with hesitant fingers
that the bind was not flimsy,
I put them on, white,
shining, waiting,
on the moodboard of
our coalescing, —
Snaking like a river
around my throat,
they waited on tiptoe
till scattered around the
bed, the flooring,
the disturbed pearls
lay happy in the breaking
apart, knew two people
had become one
in their disbanding.

All around the room
I picked them one by one,
in the hollow of my palms nestling.
Keeping each one in a box,
our memories shimmering.
