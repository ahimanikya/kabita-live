Poem Scattered by Bidyutaprava Gantayat/ ବିଦ୍ୟୁତ ପ୍ରଭା ଗନ୍ତାୟତ; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
Listlessly while
flipping through the old diary
Each time your face peeps in
through the diary pages .

The dry petals aptly slip down and scatter
Nudge to floor.
Each time the thought of you haunts me !

Over the years ,until this very moment Wandering along the blind alleys of a vague sky
I manage to strive...
Each time I miss you and confront the dark circle Underlying a lonly burning lamp
Defying the day light .

I`m chocking with exaburance but you find pleasure in my annoyance .

Each time I bleed
I scream at your indifference
I turn in to a dry rose ... That each time slips down and nudge to floor silently.
