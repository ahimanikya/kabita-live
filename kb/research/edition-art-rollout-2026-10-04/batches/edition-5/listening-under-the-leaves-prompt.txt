Poem Trees Sing by Gayatree G Lahon; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
As if they decipher the silence in my numb eyes
And I listen to the unsaid words they try to speak
They stand with their mute glances
Humans can`t perceive
But deep within I feel
As if they hum something
A melody that reaches to my ears only
The melody that they whisper to themselves
Mingles with the air
Streaming and ringing in the leaves
I can fathom why the greens are so soothing
Lost in their own thoughts
They compose a rhythm that they sing
The sky and the air resonate them
For the mechanical world they are dumb and mute
Not all have the eyes to see beyond the sight
We live in a world full of possessions
Where humans can`t even listen to the voice of their own soul
My beliefs are turning green
The emptiness that dwells in my soul finds solace
When the green leaves sway with the sweet breeze
Humming a silent mellow tune
Planting a beautiful smile all over me
