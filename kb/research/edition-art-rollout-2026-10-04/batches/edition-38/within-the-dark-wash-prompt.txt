Poem Dark Matters After All by Braja K Sorkar; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
Origin of the universe evolved from the darkness as
Life starts from the dark womb of a mother
I know nothing about the secret of this universe
Except the weave of silence
Originated from dark

A big dot created by Syed Raja
Who knew the secret I guess.
Thousands of dots arranged in this magical circle
Turned into a large dark space called Bindu.
Bindu Theke Sindu, an old Bengali proverb
Taught me how a sea is born with drops of water.
Highly appreciated painter Jackson Pullock absorbed.
All kind of colors ,created a brilliant glob rather than a universe.
I admired him most . Dark mattered to him I understood.

A shaft of light came through the dense forest of dark sky
And touched my eyes .
I woke up at midnight, listened to the music of silence
Playing around me and had a sound sleep in the eternal dark
