Full poem context only; never render its words: The Completion by Rashmi Mohapatra/ ରଶ୍ମି ମହାପାତ୍ର
Bed of thorns
Castle of love
The scenes were planning big,
The dream sequences
The set up on spring`s valley
Strokes played
Ethereal  orchestra in mind.

The reflections danced on canvas
Images had to get mended
Sometimes somewhere,
The expirations are time bound
Of animate or inanimate.

And there
Stubborn brush didn`t march on
Uncanny pallet turned face down
Coagulated rebel colours,
Had to fall a green leaf
Below the red flower
Tree  to be kind
To hold them in place
The picture to look contented
That`s the wish
Everyone`s everywhere.

From some corner of the castle
Anxious musings suddenly yelled
A punch on the bough
Caused by distracted brush
Deformed a tree
On the bank of river
Splashing waves high and low
White and blue strokes
over taking one another
Light shade got darker
And dark lighter
Session had to stop there.

The incompleteness
Completed the impression,
Started walking
On bed of gravels
The  caressing strokes
Abstract and unreal
To the castle of love and admire.
—————
Rashmi Mohapatra
Mumbai
