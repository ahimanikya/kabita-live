Full poem context only; never render its words: Future by Aneek Chatterjee
Do you see the future?
Please tell me yours,
ten years from now.

Don`t tell me about mine.
I don`t want to know.
I am happy with the sudden turn

of the river here.
I am happy with the unkempt room
and the old, injured mirror

Green leaves play
hide and seek with me
and turn brown, in front

The earth is young,
so are we. Don`t tie us
with the lure of the future

and mathematical steps.
Let the future be dark
I`ll invoke colors to illuminate.
