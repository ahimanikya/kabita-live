Poem I WALK WITHIN ME by Nandita Samanta; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
There are thousands of lonely streets within,
I Never count how many times I walked through them;
unintended, tend to linger in the unending moments between.

Time fails to weaken my perdurable journey.
My soul, as verdant as the gentle roots of an age-old tree, stands still in this solitary quarry with its deep foothold.

Light years halt in mere instances, and the moments float as light as dandelions.
Linear time is a triumph of the mind that survives in millions of goodbyes,
mirroring broken smiles that pray for an exit, but…
They come back in afterthoughts to nurture bygone time.
