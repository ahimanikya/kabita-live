Poem Crossing by Sutanuka Ghosh Roy; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
The bed-side clock chimes
the refrigerator yawns
the electric kettle calls
the rice-cooker whistles
my spine wrapped in pain
melts like mozzarella cheese.
The jelly-like knees waltz to the ringing sound of the doorbell.
The pink Jarbera smiles, the white Alamonda gigleds the fuscia Jinia chuckles
Somewhere someone whispers
it`s time to cross the shoreline.
