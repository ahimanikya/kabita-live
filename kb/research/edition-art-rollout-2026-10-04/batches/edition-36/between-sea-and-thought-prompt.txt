Poem The Mariner and the Muse by Sankar Narayan Mallick; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
I am seeking you,
ceaseless on the solitary sea,
straining Sisyphus-like
to keep the jinxes
of the journey at bay.
Sailing on and on
and dreaming of you.

With unknown origin and destination,
the ship sails on.
I look at a ballet of bobbing clouds
in a ballroom,
beautiful and blue !

A wild wayfarer am I,
not settled, civilised like you at all.
I keep wandering in the wide open seas
and wondering if I ever can
stand with you as an equal !

You shuttle between cities
and citadels of learning
with degrees and decorations,
all elegant and ashine in your urbane ways,
to which I am a stranger.

You touch me deep within,
all the same!
Elegant, yet empathetic.
Ornate, yet compassionate.
Elevated, yet so tender!

You keep walking on a track
and I, on some other
far away somewhere;
the tracks,
so different and divergent,
but dreaming always
to touch each other !

The classrooms and the seminar hall,
corridors of library,
the sprawling lawn,
the garden in full bloom,
with a book or two lying on the bench.
I can feel it all.

I keep sailing on in serenity and in storm,
in fog and mists,
light and shadow,
and under the canopy
of star-light at night.
Can you feel it ?

So much of talks and silence
to be shared between us.
I muse on as I sit at the table.
My middle finger touches the ebony top
and the glass of water on it shivers a little
In it,
there are rings and ripples.

You sit there
on the other side,
leaning on the table,
your eyes on mine,
tip of your ring finger
resting on the rim of the glass,
as the sea and sky
surround us,

A dolphin swims up
near the deck
and there is a sudden splash.
I am jolted back.

The ship sails on.
Life is an exploration.
