Poem HAPPINESS by Neelam Saxena Chandra; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
The definition of happiness
Keeps changing with time

A child finds happiness
In flying a colourful kite
Or watching an insect totter
Or seeing a bird fly in the sky
Or even getting dirty
In the muddy pool of water

The requirements of life
Keep increasing and expanding
As one grows up
And the meaning of happiness
Also gets modified
The little things
Which he so enjoyed as a kid
Seem so miniscule
The things that give him pleasure now
Are different-
Money
Fame
Social status
Number of cars which one owns
Number of apartments
Oh! The list is endless!

But as one keeps ageing further
The truth dawns upon the man
The worldly things do not matter at all
For
Everything shall go to dust...
And the definition of happiness
Alters once again....
