Poem Confession by Santasree Choudhury; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
I don`t dream anymore
I sleep to forget
I stand in front of a mirror
To find myself

My true self
I hide my feelings in a jar of dead leaves
That gives me courage to let go

Alone in a world of dilemmas
I wait and wait with lots of patience
I have succeeded to find my foes
In solitude of my changing time

A sublime phenomenon
Present is painful
Who am I to forgive

My success
that I understood
We come alone
We exit in silence

An unknown vastness of dignity
Where sparkling stars
greet with open arms...

Never ever cry for me
I will return as a yellow butterfly
