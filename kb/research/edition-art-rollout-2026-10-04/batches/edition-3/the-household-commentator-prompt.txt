Poem DRAMABAAZ by Paramita Mukherjee Mullick; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
I have a dramabaaz in my house.
Not a dog,neither a rabbit,nor a mouse.
He will roll his eyes and look at you if you his interest arouse.
Or if you give him a book, he will with full concentration browse.

When you think he is having a nap.
He is observing you with a deer stalker cap.
If I sing Rabindra Sangeet he will accompany me with a rap.
When in his good mood,he will talk like an unstoppable tap.

In our house some words are not to be uttered.
If you do, with screeching and squawking your ears will be shattered.
So we spell out words like `juice` and `ice-cream`.
Otherwise he, for his favourite foods screams.

He loves to talk to sparrows but not the crows.
Crows are his enemies so for them he has shut all friendship doors.
Never,never give his sunflower seeds to the crows, never be so bold.
He will shout at you and at the top of his voice scold.

My dramabaaz feels shy when he sees a stranger,but that`s only for a while.
Give him time,he will then say `hello` and talk to you in style.
When we were working from home and were on the computer long.
He too joined in with his chatter and song.
