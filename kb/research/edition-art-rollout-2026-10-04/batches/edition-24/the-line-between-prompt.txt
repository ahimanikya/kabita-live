Poem Crack by Raja Chakraborty; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
this crack
on the wall
thin line to begin with
searching for expanse
eating into the paint
hiding old wounds

way we
slowly drifted apart
along fault lines
our celluloid laughter
no longer bold enough
to heal
