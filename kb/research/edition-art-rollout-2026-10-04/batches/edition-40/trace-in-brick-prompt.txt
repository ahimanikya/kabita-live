Poem Tacitly So by Manas Bakshi; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
With a few dots
On a graph paper
Something`s drawn --

Remains outside
A few scrawlings
Abrupt, indistinct
Offering a clue
To an indelible story,

Story or narrative about
Some straggling moments
Saddled with the residue
Of three tangible elements:
Some bricks, letters
And trees;

Living or non-living
All`re almost the same
When extinct`s
Their tenure on earth!

Who bothers
To look backward
And recall
What these were worth?

The mason`s fingerprint
No more traceable
On the eroded bricks,

Erased also
The scurrying postman`s
Fingermark from
The useless letters
Stacked in
The dead letter office,

None cares to think
Of the felled oaks
Incapable of inviting
Rain anymore!

Erasable or perishable
Mortal or fragile
Some elements
Of impermanence
Streaked with matching colours
When in earthly existence
Get enwrapped
Today or tomorrow
In an invisible layer of time
In life`s abandoned zone....

Witness:
The graph paper alone.
