Poem Waiting by Meenakshi Mohan; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
The sun bid adieu
The Elysian in its gerua attire
the day gradually began to cease
the trees, like monks, whispered silent sermons.
the birds made homebound
squirrels and chipmunks
were safe in their abode
all was calm and reposed.
on this sedated evening
a shankhnad beyond the hills
broke the tranquility
a mystic hope in a fading dream.
far away, on a moonless, lonely night,
a light flickered from a pane
a mother`s prayers and a wife`s waiting –
each breath a prayer.
