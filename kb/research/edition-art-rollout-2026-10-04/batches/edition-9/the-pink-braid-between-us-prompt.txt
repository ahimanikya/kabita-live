Poem MY SISTER AND I COMB A DOLL`S HAIR by Jagari Mukherjee; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
We named her Starina, after a favorite
movie character.
Dressed in a glittering skirt and turquoise top, she smiled at us,
and her teeth were perfect pearls. Her long hair was a hot pink hue.

We took turns to run the tiny comb
through her rosy strands,
braiding them together with satin ribbons.
Nobody spoke a word.
She could`ve enjoyed the attention
had she been human,
but she wasn`t; she remained an enigma, her cerulean eyes open,
her lips frosted, her skin dusky:
I supposed she was surprised ;
(just as you are, just as the world is)
that two childless middle-aged women
would play with a doll.
