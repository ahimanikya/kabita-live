Poem Haiku by Daipayan Nair; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
1.
bus arrival
she steps out
of the puddle

2.
settling dusk
the tea-seller unseals
his milk packet

3.
gleaming sickle
a bard enters
the jute field

4.
deepening twilight ...
another honeycomb slice
into the bucket

5.
night street sky —
a boy spins the dough
on his fingertip

6.
two salmon
in his lave net
sunset song
7.
monsoon garden tour —
one among the group
loads his catapult

8.
the weight
of a raindrop
ant swarm

9.
Koel song
the tea-plucker adjusts
her sling bag

10.
another mound
the stink bug climbs
grandpa`s kneecap
