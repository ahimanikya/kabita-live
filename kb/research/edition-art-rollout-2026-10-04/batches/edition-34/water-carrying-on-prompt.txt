Poem Water by Toolika Rani; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
Water!
How it washes all dirt!
Without a word.

Leaves the other clean
Fills all the rubbish in its own seams
And leaves.

What motive, what reward does spur
It to clean the world?
There is no recognition, no mirth.

Emerges pure from the womb of snow
And mixes with dirt
But stops not.

It is its nature to flow forward.
