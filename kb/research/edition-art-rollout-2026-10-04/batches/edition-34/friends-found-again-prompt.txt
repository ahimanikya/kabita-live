Poem LOST AND FOUND by Dilip Mohapatra; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
There was a time
when passions subsided
like tides do
but soon they returned
but now when they retreat
they never come back.

There was a time
when you were thrilled
to hear the mindless encore
of the claque
but now the hall is empty
and the crow`s feet around
the corners of your eyes
can`t be hidden with
any make up.

A time comes
when your well wishers
ask you about your health
and your children insist on
booking the wheelchair for you
at the airport
when you rock on your recliner
on your balcony
vacantly looking at an empty sky
and feel that the emptiness within
mirrors that outside...

You wonder
what has really changed
what have you lost
along the way
and when you attempt a U turn
even down the memory lane
you get lost
in the meandering maze.

It takes some time
to realise that perhaps
it`s your youth
that has given you the slip
like sands of time
leaking through your
loose grip and
limp fingers.

You try your best
to look in every nook and corner
if you may retrieve it
once again
and someone says
age is just a number-
think young to stay young
but you know
that`s just a cliche
and doesn`t really help.

Then you receive an invite
from your old buddies
your batch mates
from your academy days
for a reunion
a get-together
in a remote island resort
and you gladly accept
and join them there
and as you meet and greet
and hug one another
in the exuberance
of the boisterous laughters
you rediscover and
find what you thought
was lost forever!
