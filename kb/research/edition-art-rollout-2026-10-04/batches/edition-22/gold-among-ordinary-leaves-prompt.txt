Poem Amaltas… by Avantika Vijay Singh; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
Nestled on branches hanging low
Heavy with the load they carry
Drooping like golden chandeliers
Are the gentle flowers of summers

Competing with the sun…
Shining on Earth
Brighter by the day
As the sun`s chariot races
Across the skies of May.

My heart swirled anew
Seeing the flowers bedecked
In their golden finery…
Like stately men in silken robes.
The same that Lord Ram saw blooming…

Awed he stood by the golden flowering
Near the hermitage of Sage Matanga
In the mountains of Rishyamukha
On his way to seek help from Sugriv
On his quest to rescue Sita.

Sugriv, the brother
Banished by Bali
For assuming the throne
When he didn`t return for 28 months
After entering a cave to fight Mayavi demon.

Sugriv, who sought refuge
in the mountains of Rishyamukha,
Where Bali was forbidden to enter by Sage Matanga
For polluting the ashram with a slain demon`s blood.
And the Amaltas flowered on Rishyamukha…
