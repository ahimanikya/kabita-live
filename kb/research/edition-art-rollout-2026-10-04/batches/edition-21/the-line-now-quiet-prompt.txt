Poem Lines by Geethanjali Dilip; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
The telephone lines that carried your voice,
Are now nesting ground for tailor and weaver birds,
All my thrills of being on the other end beyond my choice,
Now trills of their songs getting drowned with engines frightening the herds!

All the cables that infiltrate the space of earth,
Encroaching upon the privacy of tiny creatures in the soil,
That brought your laughter to my ears renewing my birth,
Now entrenched in invisible fields afar, in a circular coil.

All the decades of words that chiseled themselves to articulate,
As conversations volleying love at times inexpressible,
Tense like taut elastic bands that snap at our silence opiate,
Waking us from a reverie of our drunkenness with each other in a fable!

It doesn`t really matter as our lines are duly embedded,
Beyond the telephone lines and cables under oceans,
We have verbalized having lived promises that superseded,
Our words the very witnesses of thoughts sedimented through life`s decantations.

And conversations are now silent moments of our hands feeling each others` pulse.. Till time stops in another dimension..
