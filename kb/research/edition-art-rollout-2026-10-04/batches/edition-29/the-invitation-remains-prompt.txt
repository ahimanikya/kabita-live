Poem Invitation by Ashok Kumar Singh/ ଡ଼ଃ ଅଶୋକ କୁମାର ସିଂହ; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
Waited whole day for you
My guest!
I dosed off while waiting
In dream came my God
Also my prayers
In dream came my demon
My drowning in mud

I spread the grains
Beyond the window pane
The parrots flew away into the sky
They may come someday
Hounouring my invitation
When they trust me
And I am not in their harm`s way

Look I have kept the
Whole house lighted
My guest !
When the darkness is engulfing the street,
The distant bushes, and river front
I may jump into that darkness
To answer the bell when you come

Look at the pride I had
Inviting you, waiting to receive
The prides of hard stonewall cracked
With the tender touch of this
Indolent creeper to creep into the room
Despite her showing the way
Still you did not come

What will change
If you do not come
I will be in the room
Keep it lighted. But may forget,
that I had invited you
I may open the door
And from light, venture into darkness.
