Poem Game of Sea by Satya Sundar Samanta; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
The sandy, stony shore
Is various more!
When there is wide tide
Something to hide
Little dazzling waves appear
Embrace the shore
With a sprightly dance
They seem to be near ones.
But, when they draw back
Take away precious stones.

Then ebb comes
Naked pebbles, ribs,
Dry roots and thorny plants
On the lonely shore
When waves no more
Threaten the weary travelers.
