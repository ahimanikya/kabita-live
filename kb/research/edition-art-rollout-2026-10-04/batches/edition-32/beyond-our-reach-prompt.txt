Poem I don`t find myself by Shakuntala Gupta/ ଶକୁନ୍ତଳା ଗୁପ୍ତା; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
I arrived late,
or he came much earlier ?
How does it matters, where we land?

What if no Tulips left for us in flowerbeds
or in contour,
to cherish at this time of summer?

He made me standing
under a Southern Mangolia tree
with my wings widened,
these flowers are out of my humane reach and regrets,
he leaned shadowing me
wooing at a secret corner
like sunrays kissing a fluttering butterfly,
Floral avenue paved
arms in arms leading to heaven.

The most precious sight he laid - vulnerable part of his heart,
the floral songs-botany of love he inks
in different dialects vegan pagan lyrics reprised,
crafted with care
the surkh chinar curved motifs on me, all over
tangling my veins
veiled my vision with vanity,
since then
I don`t find myself anywhere!
