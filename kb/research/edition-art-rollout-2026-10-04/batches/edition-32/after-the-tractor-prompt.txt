Poem SCARECROW by Bharadwaj Mishra; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
Today a new tractor is itching
at the western corner
of this two acre land,
the property of Lord Lingaraj
will no more be ploughed by hand.

I will serve no job
it seems
amidst the roaring march at work
and the gigantic silhouette at night
of the machine watch-man.

I mimicked from my painted clay face
waving my straw hands,
maybe my lame feet offended the padded paw,
it crawled back and crushed
my degradable presence.

Lord Lingaraj save!
You have designed my fate
to scare away birds,
at least now send your bull to eat me all
except the skull.
