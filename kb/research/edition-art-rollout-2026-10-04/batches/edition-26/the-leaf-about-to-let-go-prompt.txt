Poem To my son by Amanita Sen; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
Because you are right there
in the middle of the rounded teardrop
that hang vulnerably
at the edge of the mother`s eyes.
I do not know the genesis of the tears.
I blame it on the pangs of motherhood-
the erratic play of hormones
mixed with the dash of a sublime joy-
to have held a wriggling bundle,
to have called it my own.

As I look back,
I see the years went by
when you were simply the breathe I inhaled a little further away from my body.
As easy, as unobtrusive like the air flowing in the lung, as inherently imperative for my soul.

I risk your eye-roll as I tell you this in a clear day of autumn,-the fall season where the trees write farewell letter to the leaves,
it was worth every moment to be called your mother ,
you are the best poem to have come to me.
