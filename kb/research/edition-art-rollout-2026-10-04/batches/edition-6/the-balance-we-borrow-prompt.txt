Poem Playing Jenga by Renette D Souza; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
The games we play to our utter dismay
In our greed for power and for fame
Leading ourselves astray, in life today
We play the Jenga game.
Our globe, she spins and pays for our sins
By those that are crooked and corrupt
Without a single thought, we take our shot
The balance of nature we disrupt.

Crippling our flora and disrupting our fauna
Polluting our oceans and seas
Thru war we reshape, flatten our landscape
And slowly we`re losing our peace.
The risks we take, put Earthly life at stake
We have only ourselves to blame
Every gamble we make, the rules we break
Whilst we play the Jenga game.

Our Earth, she crumbles as man he fumbles
Her warm green blanket he replaces
With cold cemented jungles, she rumbles and stumbles
It is a stark future that she faces.
We need to dive deep, our earth to keep
Yet we tilt the balance, ain`t that a shame
Nothing stays the same in progress`s name
We continue to play the Jenga game.

*Jenga is a game where players take turns removing one block at a time from a tower constructed of 54 blocks. Each block removed is then placed on top of the tower, creating a progressively more unstable structure.
