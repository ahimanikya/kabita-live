Bunyan Tree: Happy New Year K V Dominic

“` humans, most intelligent,
concerned of time in their life?
They greet each other good morning,
good afternoon, good evening
Today they greet happy new year.
In this infinite universe
how absurd is time and space!”
The immortal bunyan tree pondered.
She is sacred to human beings
who rest beneath her canopy leaves.
She has no death and enlarges her empire
rooting down from each branch.
She is a haven to innumerable birds and animals
and also to vagabonds and rishis.
“How idiot is a human being!” she thinks
“Always worried and sad and never content.
No meaning in his greetings;
empty words like din of a drum.
Those birds and animals are happy like me
Never bothered of time or age
Least concerned of death and departure
When one greets good morning

thousands face bad mornings,
fighting against poverty and sickness
or frightened of tortures and wars”
