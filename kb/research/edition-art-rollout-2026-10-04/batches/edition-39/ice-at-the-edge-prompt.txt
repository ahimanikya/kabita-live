Poem GLACIER by Bharati Hazarika; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
Have you seen the majestic glaciers
At the North and South Poles,
A true marvel of nature?

The world seems to be flying
A white sheet of cloth overhead;

A huge sheet of white snow,
A treasure trove of life.

But there is more to this harsh,
Cold landscape - animals thrive here,
A testament to nature`s incredible balance.

Glaciers play a crucial role
In maintaining this delicate balance,
A reminder of the importance of preserving our planet.

Now, the glaciers are melting unexpectedly,
A consequence of our actions,
The Earth is getting warmer.

The trees have been cut down, Firecrackers pollute the air zone, destroying the ozone layer.

People have dug into the heart of the earth,
Searching for gold, diamond, coal, and petrol,
Leading to more threats in earthquakes.

There is drought somewhere,
Heavy rainfall elsewhere,
Causing floods that destroy everywhere

But glaciers hold secrets -
They have suppressed many disease-causing germs ;
Hold the eggs of prehistoric creatures.

If these glaciers melt,
These germs and bacteria will come to life;
Plaguing humanity with unknown diseases.

Nature will be angry!
The remaining plants will die
due to strong sunlight.

People will succumb to heat strokes, No air conditioner will be able
to provide relief.

Science will give up;
People will cry out
for a sip of drinking water.

So, human race, if you are wise, wake up!
Remedy this situation
while there is still time

Plant trees as many as you can,
Let the earth live green
Allow the thick sheet of glaciers to be thicken

Let us breathe fresh air
O man, wake up to reality ,
Wake up….
