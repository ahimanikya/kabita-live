Poem A Complaint by Majrooh Rashid; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
You are a wandering bumblebee
flittering
from one flower to another,
and me a lotus,
shimmering on the waters of the spirit
In mid-August,
far away from your reach ,
mindful of your frenzied humming
over my petals.
Neither your wings can fly you into my breast
nor can I beseech you to stay.
I am in a paradoxical situation,
you unaware of my plight.
A strange stillness has taken over everything
I can`t even see a movement in the waters
no question of waves growing larger.
When shall you get a break
from your useless errands and absurd connects ,
and thinking of bridging the gulf
blessing me with an aura of confidence?
