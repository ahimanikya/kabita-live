Poem DUKHIRAM by Kaberi Chattopadhyaya; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
Dukhiram`s shrill voice asking for a cup of tea
breaks the silence of the morning calm...
he ,the youngest in a group of labourers eight
laboured in the capacity of an adult
sans ennui,sans disgruntlement ,sans inertia
flashing that dazzling smile of his
dukhiram, whoever named you so ?
his angora skin waist wrapped in a thin towel
his body odour permeating of pungent mustard oil
toiling under the sun ,
dukhiram was the perfect picture of enviable effulgence !
I watched him pummeling his back
with resounding slaps as he bathed...
taking more than his puny body could allow,
his inexhaustible display of masonry art
was enough to raise many an eyebrow!
savouring a plate of `panta `with the added luxury of an onion and chillies green
he cups my daughter`s kaleidoscope
and in the myriad coloured glass wonder
he discovers his lost ` childhood `!!
