Poem HERALD IN THE CHILL by Haimanti Dutta Ray; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
Season of mists and mellow fruitfulness
Is here, to warm up to –
Blossoms bloom in branches in extreme happiness
We are all gearing for the winters, as we usually do.
Birds chirping in trees amid a wintry sun,
Rising up, with half of the work left undone
Thinking whether it`s time to bring out woolens as if they`re the one
Who can ward off the chill, to fight the battle half won.
Winter is the only time for fun and laughter
Mixing of work with play comes only after –
The warmth of the sun above, as manna for aching bones
Its` fun, even in the winters, to enjoy ice cream cones.
Winters are for frolicking around –
Festival of Lights paves way for the season with cracking sound,
We, who pen dreams with words on paper,
Turn romantic and let our quills dance a caper.
Warm tea and winter mornings share a symbiotic relationship
Fog and froth are hardly as somber as Wuthering Heights` Heathcliff
The bright sun gives a clarion call to all
To come out of closed spaces, and play in the open hall
