Poem Crawling Desert by Manoj Chola; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
a desert is crawling
in to my eyes.
Like a snake returning
to its den.

All the dreams
flee from eyes
like the fearful birds
who abandoned its nest
due to heavy storm.

When the dreams die,
the eyes become barren land.
I can see the cactus are growing thickly
hither and there.

When the thorny edges
pierce in to my heart
all the emotions of humanity
drains away.

The humidity of kindness
evaporates.
The heat of love
burn in to ashes.
The snow of sympathy
never falls down
to cool down.

When the sun
with its reddy eyes
gazing at me,
the fountain of mercy dries.

I can see my life is hiding away
to some where.
I can feel my charriot of death is
arriving without any siren.
