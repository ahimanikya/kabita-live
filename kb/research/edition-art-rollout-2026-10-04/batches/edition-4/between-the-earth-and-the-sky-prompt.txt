Poem The Earth and the Sky by RANJANA SHARAN SINHA; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
< p>An invisible flight of the heart:
Slowly the earth became hazy
Roads and houses disappeared from sight,
The bundle of growing age
felt lighter all at once,
can`t tell you why!

I looked out from the airplane window:
The cotton clouds were spreading colours--
Orange, pink, turquoise, purple..
Perhaps there was a colour
beyond the rainbow, too
I don`t know the name
of that eighth colour:
My heart and me
in a universe of colours!

The heart-- an entity
divided between the earth and the sky,
A silent invitation
of the bright moon and the stars,
The heart and the mind
rush towards the sky lightning fast,
But can`t stay there
There is a string that pulls the kite
down to the ground:

The shade of dense, emerald trees,
The shower of rain drops,
The cool breeze with moisture,
And the earth`s deep blue ocean
encompassing the blue beauty
of the sky
call me back.
With the slanting rays of the evening
My being settles like a bird
in the nest!
