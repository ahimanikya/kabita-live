Poem Hopefulness by Rashmi Mohapatra/ ରଶ୍ମି ମହାପାତ୍ର; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
In the ebb of despair,
hope whispers softly,
A tide awaiting
its moment to surge,
Carrying dreams
on its crest, relentless.
Like the ocean`s rhythm, hope`s sway,
Recedes, then returns
with steadfast grace.

In the darkness of doubt, hope flickers,
A beacon amidst
the storm`s fury,
Guiding souls through tumultuous waters,
As the ebb retreats,
the tide rises anew.

Hope, and the tide,
know no bounds,
Their currents charting paths unknown,
In the ebb, both remain
silent force,
Patiently awaiting
their time to shine.

With each ebb,
the promise of resurgence,
Hope`s eternal partnering with fate ,
An ever-present reminder,
That even in the darkest hour,
Hope`s tide
will always rise again.

Oshiwara, Mumbai
