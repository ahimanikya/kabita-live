Poem Displacement by Amita Roy; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
Displacement from idyllic roots
To any obscure attic
means a lot, trying to build a whole sky
with cerulean strips
a jig saw puzzle
remembering the way it was when
dawn unburdened night`s gloom
opening up a seamless arch of blue—
it means looking for cool shade, trying to
push away the fringes of barrenness
salvaging the forsaken green,
conjoining wisps of breeze from tranquil pools,
it means defying destiny, trying
to find a fistful of fortune on a lotus leaf
licking dust of toil in laborious lanes
twisting down to disenchantment.
