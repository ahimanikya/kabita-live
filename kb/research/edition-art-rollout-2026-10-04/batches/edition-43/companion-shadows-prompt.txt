Poem Haflong by Sangeeta Dey ( Roy); source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
My midnight story

My midnights often finds me a sole audience to its feats.

Last night it’s shadows waltzed,
To the serenade played exclusively for me.

In their darkest silhouettes
I found exuberant Vibgyor.

For sometime ,I bounced in the relished freedom of silence.

Shutting the cacophonous arguments in my mind.

However, Very soon I wanted to enter the threshold of sleep and it’s dreams.

I threw a last look at the dancing shadows, in the pale moonlight.

Felt the cool of the zephyr floating from the hills,

As sleep drenched me and wrapped me in her domain.

Eyelids closed,I travelled afar.

And in that world of dreams...

Midnight shadows surfaced, Hell bent on keeping my company.
This time started the dance to a divine rendition, straight from heaven.

And with them I walked ,I soared,I flew ,I spoke.

Through the dales,
The brook, the clouds,
The rolling hills,
The fields , the thick fog
The mysterious wood.

They eased the harsh knots of reality
By their dark ,strong , steadfast company.
And help me reach nearer to
The halo of my dreams.

But someone said midnight shadows are tentacles which badly traps you in its fold.

I wonder how...
When I find my midnights as exclusive moments of introspection and reveries.
