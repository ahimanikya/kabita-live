Poem A Village at Nilagiri by Satya Sundar Samanta; source read fully, use as context only, never render the words:
Near the Autumnal foothills
Green plots of golden corns
Are separated from each other
By thin lines of wavy border
On which plentiful sticks
Grow from catkin grasses.
Beyond the fields long lines of trees
Are decorated with seasonal beauties,
Dirty dressed peasants diligently toil till
The Eye moves down the glowing hill,
Then humble peasants return homeward
When myriad feathers to their green world,
In darkness the villagers have a fear
Of wild but timid nocturnal creature
That recovers from day`s fever!
And also fear of the fair outsider!
