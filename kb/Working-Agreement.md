# Kabita Live · working agreement

1. Ahimanikya directs the redesign and retains its design/application decisions. AI supplies research, proposals, implementation and honest verification; it does not supervise people.
2. Continue already authorized work without repeating permission requests. Resolve routine reversible implementation choices; bring concrete new design options to review before applying them across journal pages.
3. Read the current dashboard, approved design system and relevant source before changes. Keep one structured work register and append meaningful events; derived views are generated.
4. The conversation “Redesign Kabita Live magazine” is the primary decision home. Browser review decisions are local evidence, not automatic publication authorization. Export them before a durable implementation handoff.
5. Preserve English-only masthead, approved fonts/colours/signature, paper and footer decisions, historical cover originals, masters, source records and licences.
6. Credit poets and translators beside their work. Consolidate design/font/art credits in the Our Story colophon, while honouring asset-specific requirements.
7. Research distinguishes sources, interpretation and proposals. Keep private material and contributor data out of public exports. Preview content is not automatically production content.
8. Match checks to the actual change. Record independent versus self-review and unresolved findings; passing checks never supplies a missing human decision.
9. KBL-DEC-005 explicitly authorizes four on-demand persona assignments; [the operating model](team/operating-model.md) defines their role handoffs. No external service, new privilege, automatic delegation or background automation is activated. Future joining, material role changes and departures follow [the lifecycle](team/lifecycle.md) and scoped human direction.
10. Keep all non-site deliverables in the KB under the [storage layout](reference/storage-layout.md), preserving old paths as needed. Keep main and portable handoff copies consistent. Preserve localhost:8771 and the old compatibility paths. Do not import unrelated video production instructions.
11. Adopt future Blueprint changes deliberately; the pinned version remains the reference until explicitly updated.
12. End work with artifact paths, status, limitations and the next review. No fixed meeting schedule, reporting cadence or continuous monitoring is implied.

These arrangements apply Blueprint’s common human-authority, provenance and review principles to this magazine redesign. [Adoption and local variations](reference/adoption.md).
