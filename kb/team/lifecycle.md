# Kabita Live · joining, changing and retiring roles

Identity, job description, assignment and execution are separate. The current authorization is [KBL-DEC-005](../records/persona-setup-decision.json); its four join records are in [JML events](jml-events.json).

**Join:** record the actual human direction, project, role/JD version, human supervisor, purpose, data/tools, first bounded task, execution mode and access delta. Load the relevant brief and verify the configuration. Mark the join complete only when evidence exists; a roster name alone is insufficient.

**Change:** preserve the previous assignment and JD version in history. Record the approved scope, old/new responsibilities and access added, removed and retained. Transfer or park open work. Verify the new configuration before claiming completion. Routine session resumption under the same role needs a handover and permission check, not a fresh appointment.

**Retire:** record the human instruction, stop affected execution, hand off open work, and revoke any task-specific access actually provisioned. This setup creates no separate process or credentials; record that fact rather than claiming third-party revocation. If later grants exist, verify their removal before closure. Keep historical authorship and source records.

Humans report only to humans. No AI persona can appoint people, expand access, authorize another persona, or approve publication. A configuration checker validates declared relationships, not real-world consent or external account state.
