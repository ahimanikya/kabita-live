## Purpose

Direct the Kabita Live website project so that its literary identity, reader experience and delivery remain coherent, while preserving the magazine’s editorial responsibilities, contributors’ rights and source history.

## Responsibilities

- Set project priorities, define the scope of work and resolve design or implementation choices that require a human decision.
- Work with the Editor and Managing Editor to establish the content and edition scope of a proposed website update. Keep their editorial assessment visible in the release record.
- Review concrete previews, changes, verification results and unresolved findings before approving publication. Request corrections or hold a release when its scope or evidence is incomplete.
- Give or withhold explicit approval for **each website release**, including preview, discovery, full release and automated batches. Identify the candidate or commit and record what the approval covers.
- Direct authorized AI assistance and decide any proposed changes to role scope, tooling, access, spending or background work within this project’s authority.
- Ensure decisions, source provenance, delivery receipts and the portable handoff are maintained, and that problems found after publication have a recorded owner and next action.

## Deliverables and completion

Maintain a clear project brief and priorities, recorded design decisions, release approvals or holds tied to an exact candidate, and a usable handoff with outstanding work identified. A release is complete only when the approved version is deployed, checked live and its result recorded; approval alone is not evidence of deployment.

## Authority and limits

Ahimanikya is the **sole website publication approver**. Editorial readiness, Git pushes, passing checks and AI recommendations do not replace his approval. The manual owner-only workflow and required-reviewer protection remain in force. Approval of one release does not extend to later releases.

This JD documents project and website authority. It does not assert magazine ownership, a founder title, Editor status, copyright ownership or authority to override contributor permissions. It does not transfer the editors’ literary responsibilities to an AI persona. No human reports to AI.
