## Questions retained

- **821:** Grandmother lineage is unspecified. Both variants remain held; Hindi speaker gender is also provisional.
- **818:** The morning-star reading of ଫୁକିଆ ତାରା remains provisional.
- **815 / 820:** The palm-leaf expression and ଶିରାସନ still require source clarification.
- **814 / 823:** Agency and speaker transitions remain open where the original itself permits uncertainty.
- **813 / 816:** Compressed buried-footsteps wording, the lichen register and grounded-farm meaning remain queries.
- **808 / 817 / 822 and the first-person ending of 818:** Existing Hindi voice-gender choices require context; this is separate from the locally repaired gender-neutral friends/student wording.
