## Source questions retained

The two unchanged held variants are **795, Gingery Care**. Its experimental expressions are not called corrected. The questions below also remain open within otherwise revised or retained poems.

- **792 (Hindi, English):** ଜାମୁଡୋଳ remains provisionally rose-apple/jamrul; confirm the local fruit name without silently changing the original.
- **801 (Hindi, English):** Source encoding damage remains unresolved: ନାହó read as ନାହିଁ, ଚିକôାର as ଚିତ୍କାର, ମନ୍ରରେ as ମନ୍ତ୍ରରେ. Confirm against an authoritative source before approval.
- **801 (Hindi, English):** ମାଈସଂଜ is rendered tender/कोमल dusk provisionally; its local nuance needs source confirmation.
- **801 (Hindi):** Hindi masculine speaker remains provisional; Odia does not specify narrator gender.
- **803 (Hindi, English):** ପକ୍ଷପାତ ରୋଗୀ remains provisionally interpreted as a paralysed patient, possibly intended ପକ୍ଷାଘାତ; original retained, source confirmation pending.
- **789 (Odia, Hindi):** Nature lookout may be a viewing place or warning; the current address asking nature to look remains unresolved.
- **789 (Odia, Hindi):** Bills is provisionally financial, and both has unspecified referents; do not supply a pair of people or birds.
- **789 (Odia, Hindi):** Opening pondside lizard / letting the heat build up / inside me leaves the perceiving/letting subject open; both current targets more explicitly assign the letting to the lizard. Confirm the intended juxtaposition before changing agency.
- **791 (Odia, Hindi):** The trusted classmate / another girl most likely names one intermediary distinct from the beloved, but the English can also be read as two. Current targets leave a two-intermediary reading possible; confirm rather than silently resolve.
- **791 (Odia, Hindi):** Later come / let us walk addresses someone after the intermediary’s confession; do not invent the beloved’s physical return or a literal reunion.
- **795 (Odia, Hindi):** Gingery cares, concave balms and proxy hug remain unresolved experimental expressions. Do not assume the spicy/sharp-care interpretation or substitute a literal surrogate hug as author-confirmed meaning.
- **795 (Odia):** Baby becomes literary ପ୍ରିୟ; its colloquial intimacy and addressee register need to be reviewed with the source intent.
- **795 (Hindi):** बदले के आलिंगन risks exchange/revenge rather than proxy; settle the intended proxy relation before final wording.
- **795 (Hindi):** Masculine speaker and the literary/feminine address प्रिये are not established by the English baby.
- **796 (Odia, Hindi):** Clock the bend has an unresolved time/turn sense; existing literal timing wording remains provisional. The source no none is read as no one without changing the original.
- **799 (Odia, Hindi):** Final I hope no land will / never mind offering contains a double negative. Current targets read the likely intended absence of objection, but source confirmation is still needed.
- **799 (Hindi):** Hindi masculine speaker remains provisional; English does not establish gender.
- **800 (Hindi):** Hindi masculine speaker remains provisional; the English narrator’s gender is unspecified.
- **802 (Hindi):** Hindi masculine speaker in छुआ/ललचाया/अकेला remains provisional; source does not specify gender.
- **804 (Odia, Hindi):** Guiding blade remains provisionally a steering oar. Confirm the intended nautical object rather than assigning a more specific part.
- **804 (Hindi):** Hindi masculine first-person voice remains provisional; English speaker gender is unspecified.
