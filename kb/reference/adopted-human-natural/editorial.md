# Human Natural editorial adoption

This unpublished Blueprint companion applies WEB-HUMAN-01 and WEB-HUMAN-02. Use the Human Natural Image and Human Natural Translation skills through the available skill system or a deliberately adopted project snapshot. Record the exact skill version/hash used; resolve resources within that skill. Each project owns its source, contextual glossary, art history, editorial holds and release decisions.

Begin with representative complete units: an image with its actual poem/article and provenance, and a full source/target translation that exercises a cultural anchor, irony, ambiguity or rhetorical turn. A structurally valid catalogue or a visual polish pass does not establish literary fidelity. Preserve completed domain queues; reference their evidence without inventing a fresh collection-wide review.

For images use Poetic Natural for literary atmosphere, Editorial Natural for an explanatory article and Documentary Natural only where evidence warrants it. Keep recognizable source identity, setting, medium and emotional purpose. Check causal materials/light, selective detail, believable gestures and negative space at normal viewing size. Beauty, a composed scene and watercolor texture are allowed; dirt, grain or darkness are not a universal recipe. An illustration need not reproduce a verse line literally. If its time, material or action could imply a contradictory reading, record the specific uncertainty rather than automatically regenerating approved art.

For translation choose Poetic Natural for poems and Reflective Prose for essays. Read the full latest source and supplied author explanations. Preserve who acts, negation, degree, recurrence, cultural anchors, ambiguity, satire and the ending's inference. Let target syntax and line breaks carry the effect without forced rhyme or glossary lines. Distinguish a faithful translation from a cultural substitution/adaptation. A bridge language never replaces the source authority. Keep word explanations separate from approval of a whole translation.

Review the image, image narrative/alt and translation separately, then together. An illustration narrative is an interpretation, not an extra source stanza or translation. Consider whether introductory commentary gives away a poem's inference prematurely. Historical prompts remain historical, even after later source clarifications; flag obsolete assumptions for future use without rewriting provenance. Label image-source and text-source hash fields precisely: an artwork master hash is not a poem fingerprint.

A project review records exact source/target/art fingerprints and algorithms, purpose/audience/mode, full-unit coverage, evidence versus interpretation, specific preserved features, actual findings, unresolved meaning and next action. Separate source query, confirmed defect, visual preference and dismissed false positive. AI self-review, fluent review, author acceptance and actual publication are different states. Never promote draft/ready/published flags on the strength of an audit alone.

Validate only relevant behavior: modified text needs source/coverage/rendering/saved-anchor checks; modified artwork needs visual/identity/composition/metadata checks. A private audit with no public edits needs record/link/hash and release-state preservation checks, not a fabricated browser or fluent-reader PASS. Keep records private, use one central AI explanation under WEB-AI-01, and retain required human/licence credits. No generation, paid tools, media upload or publication follows automatically from adopting these references.
