About this cover

The sea, held close

The shell lies where the water has left it, small against the long patience of the shore. Someone may lift it and bring it close to an ear. Whether the sound is memory or sea hardly matters in that moment. Listening has already made a distance feel smaller.

Cultural connection

A shore-found shell as a poetic object; this image makes no claim about a species, collection practice or ritual conch.
