The Girl with Four Walls

By Sulekha Sarkar

Bare-skinned she sits, in public eye, bent low,
She needs four walls, to let her power grow.
On one, she`ll paint the mountain eagle`s flight,
On next, the stormy sea, a home of light.
The third, she`ll fill with freedom, bold and free,
The fourth, a vacant space—just vacancy.
Thinking that she`s weak, do you cause such fuss?
And tear her peace, her toys, her joy from us?
Yet still she`s positive, a calm, clear breeze,
Though sad at heart, and raining tears with ease.
Clouded her mind, with thunder, mist, and rain,
She wept quite hard, and cried in yesterday`s pain.
Just look at her—the sheer relative humidity,
See how she rises, defying all rigidity.
Amidst the villagers, she bends not to the dread,
With highest-lowest temp, she lifts her head.
I watch her patterns, see her wings take flight,
I see her skill in tides, that craft and sheer delight.
