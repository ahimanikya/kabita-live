Where the hills exhale

Cloud moves across the grass and the ridge changes its mind about colour. Beyond it, another hill loosens into blue. There is no single point where the view becomes complete. The path asks only for the next few steps, and the wind makes enough room for whatever we brought with us.

Cultural connection
