Before the field wakes

Morning arrives quietly enough to hear the field breathing. The grain bends under its own ripeness while mist hides the far boundary. Someone will soon come along the path. For now, the harvest belongs to that brief hour between work remembered and work about to begin.

Cultural connection
Rice cultivation and rural mornings in Odisha; an imagined field, without assigning it to a named village.
