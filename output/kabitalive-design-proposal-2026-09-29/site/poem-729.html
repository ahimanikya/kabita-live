The field is green

By Gopikrishnan Kottoor

The field is green.
Thick with weeds, seeds
I did not ask for, waiting,
Playing for a new birth in the wind.
It must all be, because of the night rains.
The early sun probes the solitary leaf with a kiss
Such that it turns almost fluid with the see- through colour of love that is happy
To lie forever naked.
There are flowers here,
that look splendid,
but are born in misery,
such that their beauty has lost all respect,
and it is indeed strange,
that what happens with innateness,
sinks into namelessness.
Strange things happen here in the field.
Here are lives waiting,
just as if,
for your trespass upon them.
What is this Karma of small,
secret lives in hiding that glitter and wave,
or lie in camouflage all around,
in profound insignificance,
richly coloured,
wet with desires of the flesh,
yet outwardly colourless,
with eyes like ours,
stretching out to light,
a silent hunger,
and quiet breathing.
An outlier,
all masked from our lives,
that`s much more than butterfly iridescence or wasp sting, yet
earth within the earth,
in cenotaphic spaces,
secret galaxies on a cobweb,
its quiet hymns that hum on,
stirring bud, leaf, or wing,
that seem without purpose or meaning, yet, is creation on Spark.
Ferral, sublime, with continued hurt that continuously go living and dying,
beyond our sensorium,
shaping the breath of our very lives!
And,
all that we fathom are just the colours of the flowers in the wind in blossom,
the tip of the iceberg of beauty in passing.
But what makes the living and the dead,
together pulse this moment into the secret hull of life?
The field is green,
Thick with weeds, seeds
I did not ask for.

All I need now
Is a garden mower.
