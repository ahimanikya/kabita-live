Before the first line

The cup has left a small warmth beside the open notebook. Outside, the day is already underway; on the page, nothing has yet been decided. A beginning need not arrive as a declaration. It can be this modest willingness to sit down, listen, and leave a little room for words.

Cultural connection

An imagined writer's everyday table, opening the collection through attention rather than a biographical claim about an actual poet.
