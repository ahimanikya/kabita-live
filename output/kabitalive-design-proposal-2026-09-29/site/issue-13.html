About this cover

A second moon

The moon has crossed a great distance to tremble beside the reeds. Its reflection will not stay still long enough to be kept. Perhaps that is why the water feels less lonely tonight: something far away has come near, without needing to stop being far away.

Cultural connection

An imagined Odisha lagoon at night; the emotional story concerns distance and companionship.
