Shakuntala

By Gopa Nayak

I have to be born again
To tell the story of many women
Through only one woman –
`Shakuntala`

I have to be born again
To payback the price
Of love, of sacrifice
As a woman, a daughter
A beloved and a mother!

I have to be born from
The seeds of a sage
The womb of a beauty
And show the world
That neither divinity
Nor beauty shapes destiny!

I have to be married
Not with family to bless
But with cosmos to witness
The union of flesh with desire
Of love born out of pelf and power!

I have to become a mother
Without the support of my child`s father
But with the care of abundant nature
The culmination of the ethos of nurture
Without the pathos of human touch

My life will narrate the truths of humanity
That an illustrious sage can be a victim of lust
That a divine beauty can be a ruthless mother
That a passionate lover can be a forgetful father
That a righteous ruler tests claims of love and seeks evidence
That a devoted mother even in adversity shapes providence

I have to be born again
To tell the story of many women
Through only one woman –
`Shakuntala`
To give birth to sons of Bharat
They will play with tiger cubs
And learn the meaning of power
And know what it means to conquer
To be adored by nature
To be abandoned by power
And in the end `a lost and found ring` for realization
Of fleeting human frailties and fixed bonds of emotion
A story as simple as the imagination of a child
And yet profound as nature and life in the wild

The story of `Shakuntala`
Of unflinching femininity
