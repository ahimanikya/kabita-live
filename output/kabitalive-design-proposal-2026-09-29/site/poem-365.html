Path of Consciousness

By Anindita Bose

Dreams break and scatter
yet life breathes till the end

I have visions about a new
world, but at times I meet
those who doubt and ask
questions about the path
that I have chosen

I am not a poet,
Nor the whole world knows me

but there is an unseen link
between my soul and the
supreme force...

sometimes I wish I could
laugh at the follies of those
who claim to be my own

yet I cannot since deep
within me a voice keeps
chanting - `forgive who think
they can change your essence
and create a clone like their
own`

I cannot be like `you` since
my old soul has been traveling
for long seeking
reason of my existence

yet I will not disappoint
anyone, and turn my energy
into the molecules of water
to take the shape of whatever
`you` wish to see
but
the mountains do not alter at
the hope and prayers of the
travelers, yet men share the
stories of miracles and
peace witnessed at the
highest peaks.
