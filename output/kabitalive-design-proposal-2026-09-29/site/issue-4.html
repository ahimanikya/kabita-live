About this cover

A world held in a thread

Silver bends into a shape that seems too light to stay. Between its fine lines, empty space becomes part of the ornament. The maker has worked with both what is present and what is left open. A poem can be built this way too: with precision, and with room for breath.

Cultural connection

Cuttack's tarakasi, or silver filigree, provides the craft reference. The generated ornament is an original interpretation, not a documented maker's piece.
