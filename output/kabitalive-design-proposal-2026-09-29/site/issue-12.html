About this cover

The morning gathers quietly

The flowers have fallen before the house wakes. Small pale stars lie close to the earth, their orange centres holding the last warmth of night. Someone may gather them, or the breeze may move them first. Beauty has arrived without requiring that anybody be ready to receive it.

Cultural connection

Night-flowering jasmine provides the floral starting point; the image does not claim a particular local devotional practice.
