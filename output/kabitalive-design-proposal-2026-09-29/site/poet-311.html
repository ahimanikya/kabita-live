Malaya Kumar Jati

Life and writing.

Malaya Kumar Jati is an English lecturer, poet and literary critic from Odisha. His writing brings together creative verse and an interest in how language is taught and read. He is the author of the poetry collection ‘LIFE: my voice my verse’ and the bilingual grammar book ‘An Easy Way to English’.

In ‘Your Silence Speaks’, published in The Genius, silence becomes a form of intimacy that words cannot quite replace. The poem moves between delight, distance and apprehension, finding both sweetness and unease in what remains unsaid. Its address to a beloved gives these reflections a personal, lyrical shape.
