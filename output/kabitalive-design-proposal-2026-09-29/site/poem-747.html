When the Cannons Break the Sky

By Namitarani Panda

When the cannons break the sky,
Forgetting her lullabies the Earth helplessly cries
Smoke, the blackened breath of war, curls into the sky
Like a question that no one dares to answer

The borders blaze with fire
Call it patriotism, justice or victory
But the sky burns with false triumph
And the earth bleeds with sheer pain

Somewhere beneath the thunder,
a mother still hums the homecoming song
for her son’s return from the distant land
unaware of which uniform will bring him home.

War teaches the tongue of fury:
Loud, fierce and unquestioned
And the gentle dialect of compassion
Is the one we forget to speak

It lives in the soldier
Who lowers his rifle for a child
In the stranger
Who risks his life for the wounded

In the moment
when someone realises
that a heartbeat knows no nationality

Because tolerance is not weakness
It is the courage
to extend a helping hand to someone
when hatred commands you to turn away

For wars are not fought
only with bombs and bullets-
They grow quietly, sprouting from
the small betrayal of empathy

And the truest victory in any age
is not the land we conquer
but the horrible hatred
we choose to disown

So, when the cannons break the sky again,
May we answer softly-
with hearts that choose mercy
over the false triumph of hate!
