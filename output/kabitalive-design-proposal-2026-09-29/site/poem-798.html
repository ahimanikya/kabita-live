RAIN EVERYWHERE

By Snehaprava Das

Reader tools

Rain everywhere,
On the groves and gorges,
On streets and sewers,
On the thoughts
And the dull, sombre eyes,
A few stars grope blindly for a niche
To store a secret box of sparkle
In the flooding August skies.

Rain everwhere
On the heaths and  hills and houses
Clouds are ripped apart
To bleed water and woe,
Shadows go brown and then dissolve
In the muddy overflow.

Runnels of rain shyly slither
Down the window glass
A tremulous shimmer arch on a
Broken face looming over
A latent hairline crack,

Rain everywhere,
On the face in a flyblown mirror
And on the portraits of the past,
Washing the paints off
Leaving them  blurry and  faded
On the threadbare canvas.
