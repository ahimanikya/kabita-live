Arrival

By Amit Shankar Saha

In the evening I tell you
Let us write a poem tonight.
It has been really long.

We wait throughout the night
for the poem to arrive.
But nothing comes. In the morning

I decide to write
a poem about the poem
that did not arrive.

This poem is about waiting,
about a non-arrival,
about something that is not there.

You will not find in this poem
the pathos of a child sleeping
with his head on a brick as a pillow.

This poem will not play with
your sentiments or emotions.
There is a blandness about it.

In this poem nothing happens
and that is the only thing
that happens in this poem.

Perhaps this poem has been
awaiting our arrival
instead of us waiting for it.

Perhaps all poems are sleeping
with their heads on bricks as pillows
dreaming of a new poet`s arrival.
