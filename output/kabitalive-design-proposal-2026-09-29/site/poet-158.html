Ashok Kumar Singh

ଡ଼ଃ ଅଶୋକ କୁମାର ସିଂହ

Life and writing.

Ashok Kumar Singh, also published as Ashok Singha, writes poetry in Odia and English. His books include the Odia collection ଅପରାପର (Aparapara), published by Black Eagle Books in 2021. The bibliography accompanying that collection connects it with The Raging Tide and Quiet Shore and the bilingual ଦୃଶ୍ୟାନ୍ତର, titles also identified in his Kabita Live contributor note.

His poems here move between inward unease and the public world. Fear finds its subject in a television voice, weather and a falling petal. Invitation keeps a house lit for an absent guest, testing the boundary between welcome and withdrawal. In ଜଙ୍ଗଲ, a speaking forest turns human accusations of wildness back upon the people who conceal violence within it.

A few places to begin.
