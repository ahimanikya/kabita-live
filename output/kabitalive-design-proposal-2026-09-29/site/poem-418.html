The Insoluble Sum

By Matralina Pati

Time has effaced your face
Long back:
Your comely features waned
Under the sneering sun_
A pallid cluster of
Draught-bitten blooms.
Wildering sum, once,
Girdled your tender form,
You twirled your pen,
Long hence:
You aspired to figure out
The answer.
The flimsy rill of meaning
Ceased its flow.
Then,
You withered away.
On my graceless frame,
Your ebbing pain lingered.
In fear,
I retreated to a dingy shade.
I discarded my pen.
My unkempt notebook yellowed
With an injured heart.
Thus, life continues to remain
An unfathomed sum.

Deaths and the Accountant
An insidious spark of malefic delight
Lo, you could ignite the pyre of light!
Your stern frame had donned
Its dark, longish garb,
Once more:
A transient spell of twisted fun

Those eyes savoured
Handfuls of ashes.
Those fingers wielded
Their numeric crafts
On your precious ledger
Of profit and loss.
A wreckful gust in pride engrossed
And , the last lot of leaves is lost!
Green lives writhe
On the sweltering dust.
Your pen scribbles
On the timeless record
Of profit and loss
Once more:
Your dark, longish garb
Flashes the designs
Of mercantile art.
Ah! You have amassed across ages
The burnt out remanants of lives in pages
Of curated balance sheets:
In the fold of surreptitious sums
Numbers are being caged
Ad continuum.
