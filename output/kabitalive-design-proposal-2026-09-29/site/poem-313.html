A Circle - Its Bearing on my Life

By Anju kapoor

What is a circle to me?

A figure connecting me to a journey of life to death.
When a perfect embryo in a mother`s dark womb, is nurtured with maternal care,
His first infant response in his mother`s lap is playing with her sweaty hair.

Like a pupa taking a shape,
cocooned demurely under a leaf`s nape
Until it, briefly matures with its wings, as an adult butterfly ready to escape,
And in vastness of Infinite skies, it flies high,
Alas!
Until, it meets an end again yet to burn and die.

A Circle to Me is a Cycle of Life

I take a sigh of relief in my own weary self,
Basking in midst of golden halos, a divine energy felt,
I feel free in a captivating natures romance,
Gifting me with forgiveness, in midst of stoic mountains and emrald seas, In their symphonic dance.

Born from infinite seeds,
I take a shape and body, from living roots, deep under the earth,
Branching out, to bearing, a sweet tasting nectar fruit

A Circle to Me is Time Travelled

My emotions, are like, luminous tiny pearls in wandering seas,
I begin to heal in time,
Myriad of my pearly feelings woven in a chaste necklace,
A few broken, and unbroken.
Unfurling hues of silent toil,
I come out as a relentless warrior, sometimes a fallen one

A Circle to Me is Myriad Of Emotions

Circle to me is space where life is lived, pain is endured and death is welcomed
I stitch grand fabric of infinte circles where there is relentlessness, chaos and sharp edged swords.
Planning for decades yet wanting to live in the moment,
I train like a warrior, and paint on a canvas as an obsessive artist.
I urge to give myself in this burning deluge,
Spiralling and outpouring, in this timeless container when choosing Self.

A Circle to Me is Unpredictably and Movement
