MY FREEDOM

By Sukanti Mohapatra

My freedom lies in my heart
No one can snatch it away from me
It is not the body that roams free
It is the spirit of one` s existence that is boundless
Even in circumstances unexpected.

So many people across the history
Have tried to stifle the voice of soul
They had their wishes done
By creating bondage and violence superficially
There are people who remain calm
in the face of torture, threats of death
And we worship them as
Buddha, Jesus, a prophet or a Mahatma.

My freedom is the song of my soul
When words are choked it appears through my eyes
In smiles and tears, in sufferings and troubles
Through a pen, a sword, a protest, or silence.

In the sanctuary of my soul
Lies a realm untouched by chains
For there, in the quiet depths profound
true freedom is found,
Where the spirit soars, unbound by earthly ground.

No shackles of doubt, no walls of fear,
Can hinder the heart that dares to persevere.
For in the freedom of the heart, there lies a key,
Unlocking the potential of what it means to be.

I am free from the burdens that weigh the spirit down,
Free to dance in the light of life`s sweetness around
Free to embrace the beauty of each passing day,
And to journey on, unbridled, along life`s winding way.
