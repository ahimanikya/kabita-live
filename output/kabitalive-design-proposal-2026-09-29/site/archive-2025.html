Story behind the cover · Issue 38

Enough light for one room

The lamp does not try to illuminate the entire night. It keeps one small circle warm: a wall, a pair of waiting hands, the place where a story might be told. Outside, darkness continues. Inside, there is enough light for someone to feel that they have been expected.

Cultural connection
An everyday earthen oil lamp, approached through intimacy rather than a claim about a particular ceremony.

Story behind the cover · Issue 37

A boat for remembering

A small boat leaves a large wish behind it. On the water, paper becomes a traveller for a moment, carrying what the hands could not keep. The shore is still close, yet departure has already begun. Memory sometimes needs only a little current to find its way forward.

Cultural connection
A poetic reference to Kartika boat-floating traditions in Odisha; an imagined contemporary paper boat, not a reconstruction of an ancient vessel.

October 2025

Story behind the cover · Issue 36

The season in white

Along the path, pale plumes catch the light that the day is slowly losing. No calendar hangs in this field, yet the grasses know something has changed. They lean together without agreeing on a direction. The season passes through them like news spoken very gently.

Cultural connection
Kans grass and the changing seasonal landscape; no single festival is asserted by the image.

September 2025

Story behind the cover · Issue 35

Not yet a flower

The bud keeps its colour folded close. Around it, water holds an imperfect sky and the broad leaves of yesterday. There is no need to hurry the opening. Some poems, like some affections, become themselves by remaining quiet until the right light finds them.

Cultural connection
Lotus and pond life, familiar across Odisha and beyond; the narrative concerns anticipation rather than ritual symbolism.

Story behind the cover · Issue 34

Rain on the tiles

The tiles have darkened under the rain, each small curve carrying water towards the courtyard. Beneath their edge, a cloth stays dry. A roof is made of many ordinary pieces, yet together they keep a little space for life to continue at its own pace, sheltered from the weather.

Cultural connection
Terracotta roof tiles and a sheltered courtyard offer a study of everyday domestic architecture in Odisha.

The bowl holds a note

The hammer rests, but the bowl seems to remember its sound. Light follows the faint circles left by the maker, moving slowly towards the empty centre. Soon there may be a meal here. For a moment, the vessel holds something less visible: the rhythm by which it came into being.

Cultural connection
Kantilo has a brass and bell-metal craft tradition.

When the lane turns fragrant

The first rain reaches the lane before anyone steps outside. Dust darkens; the air becomes familiar in a way no photograph can hold. A door remains half open. Somewhere within, a sentence is left unfinished because everyone has recognised the same fragrance at once.

Cultural connection
The scent of rain on earth anchors the brand's ମାଟିର ମହକ theme through everyday experience.

Story behind the cover · Issue 31

Summer, carried home

The basket is heavier on the return journey. Beneath the leaves, summer has gathered its sweetness into uneven yellow shapes. At home, someone will choose the ripest one for somebody else. A season becomes a memory through such small arrangements of generosity, long before anyone calls them poetry.

Cultural connection
Mango season and the everyday culture of bringing food home; no named cultivar or local origin is claimed.

Story behind the cover · Issue 30

Salt in the cotton

The gamucha dries slowly in the sea air. It has known shoulders, water, a hurried hand; now the breeze lifts one corner as though turning a page. Nothing precious has been put away. The day's tenderness hangs here in plain sight, woven into an object used and used again.

Cultural connection
Puri gamucha is the user's everyday cultural reference.

Story behind the cover · Issue 29

What the woodland holds

The path is scarcely wider than a thought. Kendu leaves hold their quiet above it while last season's fallen leaves soften every step. The woodland is neither empty nor waiting to be discovered. It carries its own daily life, and the lives of people who already know its shade.

Cultural connection
Kendu woodland generally, as clarified by the user.

Story behind the cover · Issue 28

The sari keeps the river

A sari rests in folds, but its patterns are still travelling. Along the border, colour meets colour like a river meeting another season. Someone will lift it soon, and the room will lose this small landscape. The cloth will carry it elsewhere, close to the warmth of a living body.

Cultural connection
Sambalpuri bandha is a tie-and-dye weaving tradition.

Sambalpur district · Handicraft (external link, opens in a new tab)

Story behind the cover · Issue 27

The net before morning

The net lies gathered beside the boat, holding only the light for now. Every knot belongs to a task repeated so often that the hands can remember it without looking. The water has made no promises. Still, preparation goes on: an ordinary form of hope with work inside it.

Cultural connection
Coastal fishing work provides the setting; the scene avoids assigning a livelihood or identity to an invented named community.
