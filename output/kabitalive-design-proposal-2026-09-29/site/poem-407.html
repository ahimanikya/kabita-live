ABRACADABRA

By Soumya Sephali Sahoo

In a blink of an eye
A dove flies out of his pockets.
In just microseconds
Live bodies are split bloodless .
Playing cards turn into roses
More and more magic he discloses.

Chained boxes and iron chests
Pricked with swords and axes
Inside people still safe and
In the hall resound applauses.
The magician utters some spells
and Shows tricks without causes.

I wish to gain all his power
To learn all spells and tricks
and say Abracadabra to vanish
And then observe people
Who is my friend, who an enemy,
And see myself astonished.

I want the magic wand to confer
Peace on all war prone zones.
To teach unity to all the distant
Families, friends and couples.
Then convert all guile, avarice,
and Doubts into bubbles.

I can then change weapons into
Books and flower bouquets.
And turn guns into pens
and bombs into buffets .
I will change abuses into good
Wishes and rage prohibited.

I will then conquer the blues
Of evils and triumph over
insanity and inhumanity and
Make the world a four leaf Clover.
I`ll Burst the balloon of racism
and Bring all colours closer .
