I WILL FIGHT BUT NOT QUIT

By Sonia Gupta

O’ every moment I fight with my hurdles,
I am a woman who wishes to cross obstacles,
I know, I am surrounded by problems,
Yet I believe in burying them in the ground.

I do not bother about any storm coming,
I will keep on walking and moving,
Igniting my courage and strength within,
I will fight and I believe to win.

Yes, I know I am all alone in my life,
Yet I will keep on struggling for strife,
I won’t shed my tears anymore,
Life is a challenge and I will adore.

I do not get afraid of the thorny paths now,
Even in failure, I say O’ wow,
I learn from my failures and move on,
I am a woman who fights with special weapons.

Come on, O’ life, bring to me hurdles,
I too promise that I won’t quit and will bring miracles,
I am fighting and will fight till the end,
For me, my inner strength is my best friend.
