NIRVANA

By Dola Dutta Roy

I`ve travelled far and wide,
Breathless and
Wide-eyed,
Looking for the luminous light.

Empty handed,
in disbelief,
I stare at the dancing beams of the moon --
on the waves
of the dark blue ocean.
Looking for a destiny.
Unable to stir,
I stand,
Watching the passage of time, in silence;
just waiting.

In the soul-less raging storms,
I fight fear.

In the purple shadows on the dirt roads,
Greed churns in my belly.
Fear burns in my eyes.
I hear the flutter of hate
In my heart beat.

When night falls gingerly,
like a fool,
I look for the sparks in the roadside taverns,
in the midst of deafening laughter
and the clinking of glasses;
Perhaps, this is where
Men and women
sell their souls
for a moment of glory.
Yet I look for a face here.

In a moment, with a jerk,
I turn away.
I cover my eyes from burning.

My sadness deepens.

So I run to seek answers
Among traveling gypsies
Who speak in riddles,
Sing in foreign tongues ,
And speak in the voice of mystics.
They laugh at me
And I learn little.

I walk for miles,
Like a woman possessed,
Able to move only in a swagger.
I stagger and fall.
My stomach, empty,
My hair in knots.
My skin, in ugly wrinkles.

With hands folded.
In the depth of woods,
I weep in silence,
Waiting for the light
That could wash me clean
And show me the way.

Perhaps, I hear a thousand bells ringing in my heart;
A thousand petals opening up,
all at once ...
Do I feel the rhythm of the chakras moving,
swirling and singing,
desperate,
to escape from within!

I drop to the ground,
crazy and feeble.
I laugh and weep --
As I feel, I`ve found the light.

This is where I must rest
my head.
This is where I must seek my long awaited silence.
My solace,
My N- i-r-v-a-n-a !!
