No Love Poems Henceforth

By Bishnu Charan Parida

No love poems, henceforth,
Nether I will write
Nor I will do read love poems any further

But it`s not easy for me
As a lover, I call myself,
Ants to elephants
Seas to skies, all that I know,
I have been in love with

Through the streets of love
I have passed,
I loved people, who returned me with pain
Scars I got in me, heartbroken, devastated

But, never had I stopped loving
Because a lover I am,
Even though they hated me
I loved them

I have always been
Part of a love story, not that of hatred,
I love, I hardly hate
