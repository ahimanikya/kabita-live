Abandoned Homes

By Heba Fatima

Abandoned homes are always finished
Dust returning to dust
Jambs with missing hinges witness the fall of fallen things
Words get stuck in telephone coils
sighs pass through borders.
Trespassers.

Hard, plastic monkeys get crushed
They don`t beat their drums
Teddy bear arms live without their bodies or their owners
Tears debris blood debris charred bones debris
A set of false teeth debris
homes debris
homelands debris.
