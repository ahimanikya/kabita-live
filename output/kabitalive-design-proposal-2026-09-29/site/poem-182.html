Ashes

By Ananya Goswami

I hear the thousand waves
crashing on the sand
all night
windswept leaves
incessant susurration
iridescent dreams of the earth
swept and buried into
the heart of darkness
I see the skeletons of poor souls
laying on the sand
weeping,
sighing
wallowing
in the darkness

I see the pain
Feel the wound...
the storm
the burning ember in your heart

I know what it means
to be in pain
I know what it means
to die a thousand deaths
and wake up to the same world
over and over and over again...

I see your heart
the endless emptiness
I feel
the warmth of your tear
I hear
your raw words
wrapped and then unwrapped
in rhythmic art of poesy

I see ashes of broken dreams
floating
in the air
falling down
on the surface of
the ocean deep and dark

I see your deer eyes
quest for the ashes
quest for a new life
to reborn like the mythical bird

but the ashes won`t always
make you Phoneix
sometimes it`ll be the old you
the broken you
who will have to
curve the path through hell..
to keep on living.
