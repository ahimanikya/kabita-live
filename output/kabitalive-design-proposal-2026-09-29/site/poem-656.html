Autumn Returning

By Devika Tripathy

In the hues of amber dusk,
leaves surrender,
like whispered vows, trembling, slow..
each one a page from a story we almost wrote.
We loved in the pauses between seconds,
a flicker of breath over chai’s steams,
your laughter a chime in the quietude of mornings,
where syllables danced like dust fairies in Sun,
untouched, yet felt.
Conversations bled past midnight
into the silver silhouettes of 3 AM,
our words not seeking answers,
just company in the ache.
It wasn’t the fire, it was gravity.
Quiet, undeniable,
pulling stars closer
without ever asking them to fall.
You touched nothing of me
and yet held it all.
Friendship: the gentlest of thieves.
We wandered through memory like smoke
..hands brushing in the dark,
not holding,
just knowing.
That was enough, and not nearly.
I keep the jingle of your laughter
like wind through bare trees,
and your silences
where I built my hopes like fragile frost.
Autumn teaches the art of letting go..
but never promises it won’t ache.
Some leaves cling too long.
Some hearts too.
My love for you,
like the stillness of Autumn rain,
and morning’s light on a window,
deep,
quiet,
unreturned,
but never lost.
