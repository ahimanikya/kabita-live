Pradeep Biswal

Pradeep Biswal serves as Editor of the journal.

Words that cross between languages.

Pradeep Biswal writes poetry in both Odia and English. His literary work also includes translation and editing, and his poems have travelled into other Indian languages. At Kabita Live, where he is listed as Editor, that bilingual practice sits naturally within a journal that welcomes Odia, Hindi and English together.

His Odia collections include Bhumiparasha, Dhusara Dhrupadi, Seshaloka, Prema Kavita and Pheriba Batare. Setu’s author profile also records Jal Darpan and Lautte Raste Mein, two collections of his poetry in Hindi translation. These books offer several paths into his writing, through the original language and through the work of translation.

The inward house, the world outside.

A House Within, published by Authorspress in 2018, is an English collection that offers a useful starting point. In his review, Bhaskaranand Jha Bhaskar discusses its attention to nature, love, introspection and social concerns. The book’s title becomes a way of thinking about inward life: the feelings and contradictions that remain within the places we inhabit.

The review also describes a poetry alert to the public world, including war, hardship and the lives of ordinary people. Its account presents contemplation and social observation as neighbouring concerns, rather than separate compartments of the poet’s work.

A life in words and public service.

Alongside his literary career, Biswal served in the Indian Administrative Service. His own biographical note records his retirement as Special Secretary in Odisha’s Finance Department. Poetry, translation and editorial work form the continuing literary thread through that public-service background.
