Humming Of Faith

By Ranjit Dutta

As some abysmal faith
hypnotizes us
I know
Why am I alone,

you know that too
We are on the radius of a circle
Moves from centre to circumference
God never gives unasked
Action has result
That every one knows
Either a fear of losing or
A hope to getting
Makes us humble or so unknowingly
God will not give you,
He will never give anything to anyone
We are causes
We are effects
And sometimes
Succinct of cause and effects.
Is there God in vacuum?
In air having no atom
Does faith runs fast
When dreams burn in fire?
Even faith is Oasis of life
Nectar for thirst
A wrathful wassailer of
Evaluating light
A victory song of
Colorful mirages.
In oscillation of trust
Dreams bloom
In humming of faith.
Life burns.
