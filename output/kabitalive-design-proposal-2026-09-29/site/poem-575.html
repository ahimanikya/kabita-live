Beauty with Brain

By Pallabi Das

Reader tools

You are so marvelous
I am speach less to narrate in words
You are full of brain
You are centre of attraction for everyone
That is you only,no else
You are only mother.

Amidst your beauty
You have created
In such a flawless manner
Who ever has taken shelter
With the genius
blessed upon by you
They have set their feet on the moon.

As strong he is and
genius as well
They could be no other than our beloved Bikram and Pragyan
Wandering on the moon
Seeking for water and air
Transmitting message day by day on exploration
With the intellect blessed upon by you.

They have already arrived there
Thus have they up held your name in highest esteem
Oh, mother you are
As infinite as heaven
I am simply numb in my speech
Singing your glory as ever.
