A threshold in rain

Someone drew a welcome before the clouds arrived. Now water softens the white lines, carrying a little of the doorway into the earth. The welcome survives its own fading. Tomorrow a hand may draw it again, because some forms of care are made to be renewed.

Cultural connection

Jhoti and chita provide the visual reference: restrained white threshold drawing within an imagined earthen home.
