The loom remembers a voice

A thread crosses another, then another, until the empty space can be held in the hands. The loom makes a rhythm without raising its voice. This imagined tribute places poetry beside patient work: a life finding music in repeated gestures, and a language finding strength in its own people.

Cultural connection

An imaginative homage to Gangadhar Meher, the Odia poet born into a weaver family in Barpali. This is not his documented loom or workshop.
