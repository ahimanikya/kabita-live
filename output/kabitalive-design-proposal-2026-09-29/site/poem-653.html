GOSSAMER DREAMS

By Leena Thampi

Amidst the throngs of faces, one stood out so fair,
The one I missed, oh ! how I wish I could repair,
His eyes like stars, his smile like sunshine bright,
The one I missed, my heart still takes flight.

A heart so full of hope and cheer,
A love so strong, so pure and clear,
The memories of moments we shared,
Are etched in my mind, forever bared.

The thought of him, it still haunts me,
A longing so intense, it still taunts me,
Will he come back, and set me free?
Or will my heart remain captive, you see?

The days go by, and I still wait,
For the day he`ll walk back through the gate,
But time keeps passing, and I`m still here,
Left to wonder, left to fear.

The nights are long, and dark, and cold,
My heart is heavy, my soul is old,
I miss him so, it`s like a pain,
A pain that words can`t explain.

So I wait, and hope, and pray,
That he`ll come back, some day,
And bring with him, a brighter day,
And chase the shadows away.

His soul, a gem, a treasure to behold
His love, a fire, that never grows old
His strength, a mountain, steadfast and true
His kindness, a river, flowing pure and through

In slumber`s embrace, I find thee there,
A vision of beauty, beyond compare.
Thy visage, a canvas of grace,
Adorned with charm, in every place.

Thy lips, a rose`s gentle hue,
Inviting all, to love anew.
Thy eyes, like pools of crystal deep,
Reflecting all, that`s pure and sweet.

Thou art a dream, a fleeting thought,
A moment`s joy, a love brought.
Thy touch, a spark, that sets my soul aflame,
And in thy presence, I am not the same.

But alas!thou art a dream, no more,
A fleeting vision, on the wind`s shore.
And when I wake, thou art but a memory,

A longing, that doth haunt my mind, so dreary.
