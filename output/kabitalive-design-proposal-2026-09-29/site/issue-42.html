The wind reads first

At the edge of the sea, the trees never finish their sentence. Wind moves through the thin leaves, carrying salt further inland. A person could stand here with nothing to say and still be understood. The shore listens with its whole length, then gives the silence back.

Cultural connection

Odisha's coastal landscape, interpreted through casuarina, sand and sea air.
