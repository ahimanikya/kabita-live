Winter

By Sukanti Mohapatra

When I was out on the road
Searching for warmth of a morning
I entered into an alley
Where winter had set in
A poster was declaring a girl missing.
A newspaper was flying over a drunken head
Hearts were found very old and cold.
`This is a blind alleyway`, someone said
`No, a jungle path`, cooed another
A growl was heard, a little lamb butchered.
`This is real winter`, I felt.
I wanted to return but fog was dense
A pub was full of revelry but nobody had sense
They were burning some flesh to keep them warm
What a strange place, without any charm?
Because this is winter no flowers bloom
Air is stagnant, everywhere the gloom
Has spread its wings to cover the earth
Ohh.. we need a heart to give us warmth
Life recedes back to meet its death.

Winter comes to a No-man`s land
Where people are hiding from sun
And night is soaked with blood.
Winter is the blanket they need
Thick mist that covers their plight
Or sight from the perpetrator of crime.
Winter arrives at the door of a widow
Whose white veil has pores
She seeks warms from a hearth
Whose fire is cooled by a downpour.
Winter in a shelter house rests
With restless orphans lying in empty stomach
Winter is kinder than spring
It lulls them to endless sleep.
