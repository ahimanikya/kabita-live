Song of Stone

By Gayatri Das

O Sculptor!

On a mild winter night
A copper coloured moon like your shade
Bathing in the sea.
I pluck the wet rainbow
In a handful water,
He who turns into a white swan
With the touch of my finger.
Feet, wings, neck, eyes
Everything is alike yours!

He then splashes the handful of water again
Towards the mirror facing the sky
The mirror returns a red fish.
Palms, navel, chest, lips
as soft as I
An elegant - Red fish.

The white swan looks with flashing eyes,
Between seeing and not seeing
In the waves of body and folded expansion of the mind
And the red fish floats on the edge of the mirror.

As if a saree is spread all along the ground
The earth is wrapped in vines, branches and leaves
flowers, pollen, twigs
Stalks, petals, hives
The carefully inscribed intricate landscape.
Entire sky is bowing down
lying on the lap,
Red fish blushes in the mirror.
Dark shades of white swan
Moves away from itself.
Where is the shade?
Lies, lies... lies
A mirror always lies.

O Sculptor...

The picture of the crafted chest
Washed away the shyness from the sublime red fish
Red fish Faintly smiles are like silver coins
in the tail
The drunken sea yawns.

The tidal moonlit night is dancing
in the eyes of the white swan,
On the layered water bed
Quietly sleeping the fascinating - Maunavati.
The ocean melts inside the mirror,
The red fish backstrokes in the sea.
The white swan kisses the mirror
Mirror in passion
Who never has a body of its own.

O Sculptor!

Winter is coming to an end.
The red fish is returning into the aquarium
The sea is silent with all its tears
The white swan cannot see the perimeter of the aquarium
Aquatic plants of a millennium are in his eyes!
