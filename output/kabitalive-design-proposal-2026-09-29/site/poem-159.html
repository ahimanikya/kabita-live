Woman interrupted

By Sonali Pattnaik

woman interrupted is woman radicalized
she bears witness irrespective of the skies
she has roamed and soared
how she is told that she must not slide
out an inch from the single tiled space
that she has been assigned
spaces, she is reminded again and again
are by default male, she must keep
herself loosely ungendered when she claims
ground beneath her feet, unrecognizable
her words must appear so they can be unheard
from the already colonized speech
she must quickly present and quicker leave
if recognition she claims
then the terms are made clear again and again
she must not be woman
if she is, she must be praised by men
applause is reserved for them
resoundingly it beats its disavowal into her heart
as she is met with speechlessness and awe
when she is done speaking her part
those things, she has come to understand,
are the double-bind of minds that have been taught
to whistle, hoot and clap at male glory alone
and remain torn when presented
with the discomfiting fact
that women can be heroic,
perhaps, more by far.

eagles or doves they find
that mere ropes strung across
the ground they are asked to walk
can stop them in their tracks
but a woman interrupted is a woman radicalized
she will pick up the axe the next time
you trip her up by explaining to her
that history or the stage are male prerogatives
and in mid-flight interject
that she is running out of time,
she will hack all that binds so the ground becomes sky
seize your bullets as they face her and fill her pens
with their powder and fire out her lines
she will come back for the mic
and drench the stage with her voice-blood
finish your lies, her body all tongue and eyes
it is earth that hath no fury like a woman denied
woman interrupted is woman radicalized
