Passing

By Mahua Sen

I fold the epoch of peace
into the percale of my red saree.
I purloin the half-baked sun-rays
to bespangle the fabric,
and I happily flaunt it
that breathes with the
remnants of my humpbacked breaths,
tending to the alizarin celebration
of a life well lived.
I scour the umbra of the void,
morphing it into amber
to match my hazel iris .
A circadian rhythm of dark and light.
My exhalations ,
the waiting arms of a choir !
I hear the azaan ,
the church bell
I hear the gong of the temple
I hear the Buddhist chants
the Amrit Vaani from the Gurdwara.
A perpetual incantation rests on the
pink of my lips.
I unlock my secret diary
where memories prostrate on the
atom of every dust .
I sit there, with those memories,
or with the dusts perhaps !
I devour nostalgia, until it reaches
the crescendo of silence.
I get marooned somewhere between the
edifice of happiness and despair.
I open my old album
lifting the veil of a thousand yesterdays.
I read those eyes laden with blue kohl ,
skin reflecting the astral shimmer
reminiscing moments of celebration
with He, She and Them!
A tempest churning in the pit of my stomach.
I run my fingers through the pictures
of granny, grandpa, uncle, cousin-
They dwell in another realm.

I whisper “ See you soon`` !

I open my laptop
I skim through the hundreds of poems that
hyperventilate in a folder  I`ve named “ Alter ego”
I whisper “ sorry” -
for most of them could not see
the light of the day!
The folder being their panopticon!
I open my treasure trove
that houses my children`s
souvenirs.
A riot of emotions playing backgammon
with the aorta of my heart.
Deluge of tears make my vision amaurotic.
Their first card to me, their letters,
Tiny chits with jumbled handwriting
“ I love you mommy”
“ Come back soon from office, I miss you``
“ You`re my trophy mommy”
The glorious gallimaufry moistens my eyes!
I whisper “ May you never lose your sense of wonder” !

I pick up my guitar for the last time
adjust the capo
I play my favourite tune
“ City of starts are you shining just for me``
I peep outside my window
The sky sighs in melancholia,
I trace a sparkling star, Sirius perhaps?
The cryptic silence bleeds from the strings of my guitar,
giving birth to another tune that I do not know of ;
baring my soul to my bosom friend, my room!

The inchoate emptiness evaporates
in the dichotomy of moments !
A chaotic calm annotates a mortal fizz.
Now I`m ready to embrace a life
beyond this life !
For I do not like the colour of fear.
It does not enhance the complexion on my
consciousness.
I await a perfect marquee without dimension
I await the dusk when shadows merge with
the asphalt of the night.

All I can hear is stillness,
rubbing itself against my pale body.

Will I really die when I die?
