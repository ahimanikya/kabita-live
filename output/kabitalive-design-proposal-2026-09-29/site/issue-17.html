About this cover

A forest breathing softly

The forest does not need a grand entrance. A fern uncurls beside water; a small blossom finds space on a dark trunk. Further in, the trees gather their own weather. To look closely here is to discover that wonder often begins below the level at which we usually search for it.

Cultural connection

Similipal's forests, streams, orchids and ferns ground this imagined ecological study. It is not a documentary photograph of a surveyed spot or a verified orchid species.
