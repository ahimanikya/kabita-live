Artwork and image sources

The homepage artwork LIFE AS IT IS, inner-page opening illustrations, poem illustrations, paper textures and 47 magazine cover artworks were created with AI assistance for this redesign. They are artistic interpretations of place, culture and everyday life, rather than documentary photographs.

The poem illustrations accompany ଦୋକାନ, क्षणिका and Drink. Individual cover narratives and cultural references remain with each issue. The Pipili Chandua study is reserved for future use.

The editors’ portraits use existing published photographs with an AI-assisted paper and watercolour treatment. Their original image sources and biographical references are recorded below.
