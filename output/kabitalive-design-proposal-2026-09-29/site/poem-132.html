Troubled Dawn

By Dipak Samantarai

Strangely
dawn
is late

The Sky
hesitates to
cast off
it`s nightwear

Birds
are restless
wanting to leave
their nests

A mother
reaches out
for a little more sleep
While the world
comes to a stop

Sorrows
hover about everywhere
until they
finally find it
under their microscopes

A ball
pink in colour
with needle-like spikes
prickly all around

Looking
at their frightened faces
it laughed and laughed
echoes drowning
the screams
of the dying
and the dead.
