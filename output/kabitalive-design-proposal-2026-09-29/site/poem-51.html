MEMORIES

By Jernail Singh Anand

It is always in the sea of memories
Where I keep afloat
And it is the time slot
Which brings back
Events happened in the past.

I wonder about the times to come
Living in a house,
Of liquid material,
The paste of the Past,
My memories sweet

Whenever alone, I slink back
Into the cosy lap
And how
Crying aloud
They call me back.

How alive they are!
Past, you are not described properly.
Thou art past continuous
Flowing through the present
Into the future.

Trains have systems which show
Which cities and stations
They have passed
Unlike our minds,
Who carry along the stations left behind.

Very few are the moments
Which drag me
From the pool of the past,
And see me ramp walk
In a swim suit

After ambling about all day,
As dogs to their kennel retire
So doth I fling back
To the chawls
Where those shadows abide.
