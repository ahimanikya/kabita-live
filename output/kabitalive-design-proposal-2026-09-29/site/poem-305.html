WHAT COULD I EVEN WRITE ABOUT THIS EVENING?

By Suchismita Ghoshal

Reader tools

What could I even write about this evening?
I could see the sunset dispersing in the sky,
leaves gently brushing themselves off,
a gentle breeze is going forward,
hundreds of vehicles and humans passing by,
and me, waiting to see an entire day ready to bid adieu.
Things have been dismantled,
a beautiful day is lost among thousand mid-youth worries,
another day has passed without living through it completely.
These days are fully bland, offering many things
but I am too full to accept what life should be like.
While being in the alleys of careeristic opportunities, sunsets are getting missed.
Even the evening tea doesn`t feel like home thousands of kilometers away!
Two mid-aged human beings are waiting and getting themselves arrested with the handcuffs of senility.
Their eyes are waiting to see their small `girl` growing into a completely earning `woman`.
The complete woman here loses all her childish dreams to elevate her skills all day,
to earn, to learn and getting dismissed in the ocean of crowd.
We have only this moment in our hands
and we lose ourselves in the horizon of oblivion.
The present pats and says, Good job buddy, how many evenings are yet to be lived in uncertainty
I know no answer, probably will look at the present and a long sigh may complete the rest!
