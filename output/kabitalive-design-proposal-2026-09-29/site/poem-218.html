The Water Of Life

By Kakoli Ghosh

Distilled to live through blank breaths
aged until perfect, in casks of death,
treasured poison turns into elixir,
melancholy brews the salt of rain.

Dying is just a regular habit of life,
living is kept sealed as firewater
in a glass bottle kept away from sun.
Fulfilled grief bubbles in poured fun.

A smile wrought with a hidden sigh
yearns through ages of forgetting lies,
an immense pain grinding the stars,
sells naked nights at a sky high price.

Memories sip the aroma of old pain,
whore lips shape the crescent moon,
the wet mind soaks into the pillow,
ice cubes drown drunk babbles soon.

Golden solitude, poured on the rocks
intoxicates curtains and door locks;
when death gets sold at any price,
an uncertain life, who`ll borrow twice?

Selling leftover care, death earns life,
stray love licks careless dreams,spilt;
late moment runs gasping after time,
stares blank at the empty railway line.

Wheeled wagons of enslaved time
leave the platform clanking in rhyme,
learnt multiplication tables repeat
in a harmony everytime like a chime.

The white chest pocket retains
the smudged marks of vintage wine,
aborted pain bears a washable name,
that outlives the aroma of dry stains.
