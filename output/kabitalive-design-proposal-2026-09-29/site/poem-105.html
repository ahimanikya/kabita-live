Present—Trench—Future

By Agnivesh Mahapatra

Once, I said to one...
There is always
A trench...
Not narrow, but broad
Filled with nothingness;
A vacuum, a black hole.
Between
The Present
And
The Future.

The Present...
What is.
What one is.
What one does.

The Future...
What is wished, dreamt, expected.
What one wants to be.
What one wants to be doing.

In one`s life,
A time comes
When one falls
Off the cliff
Into this deep, emptiness.
No way out.
One gets stuck;
In monotony, routine.
World calls it settlement.

I was asked: Sir! How to get out?
I said: Quite easy...
When in nowhere, be a nobody.
Accept the Nothingness.
That`s how, `ChandAshoka` turned `DharmAshoka`.

I saw a hapless smile.
That`s it? He asked.
No. I replied.
Either that, Or
You manufacture a Future
Out of your Self; Full of yourself.
That`s how, Siddhartha became Shakyamuni.

Can`t one prevent this misery,
Not falling into it at all?
The inquisitive one kept on...
I said... Certainly one can.
There is a rope;
Of your dreams and aspirations
Thin, sharp and long.
Connecting the present and future.

Walk on it, steady;
Full of faith, determined.
Putting in effort, constant.
Don`t stop, till
What you are, becomes
What you wanted to be.
Though, I am saddened to say;
Most of us, end up leading
A settled life, in those dark depths.

I won`t fall, Sir!
I heard a voice, confident.
I smiled, now content.
