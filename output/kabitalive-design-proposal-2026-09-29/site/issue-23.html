The sky comes to the field

Water settles between the young rice plants and brings the sky close enough to touch. A cloud breaks into pieces when the wind passes. The field is both labour and reflection, both what must be tended and what cannot be held. For a moment, the two share the same light.

Cultural connection

Flooded paddy fields provide the Odisha agrarian reference; this is an imagined scene, not a record of a particular farm.
