MOVING IN

By Sharanya

My cold fingerprints on
The untouched switchboard

Freshly cemented walls
Grey like a promising sky for
A drought-ridden land

Blue, Red
Tangled electric cords, intertwined
Streaming along the dusty
Corners of a marbled floor

Stained buckets with paint to the rim
Glazing, but too reluctant to reflect

Battling their piercing chemical scent
I hope, hopelessly for
A faraway gaze
Rippling towards me like silk

And a calling
Pungent yet Musical
Like a vintage telephone-ring

Your holographic vision coils around me
Non-stop and
I struggle for breath in an empty room

My hair now damp and
Drenched in blue

Bless my delusional head
Pleasantly, bravely
Housing more picturesque worries
Than I can ever afford

For I have run out of places to move into.
