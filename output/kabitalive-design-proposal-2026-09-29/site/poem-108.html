Rain Now and Then

By Anadi Charan Pradhan

Rain does not descend
as naturally as it used to
When I was a child.

It`s now its whims and pleasure
To drizzle when mother earth desperately needs a downpour
Fueling the unending agony of the hapless farmers.

Its anger inscrutable
As if fumes hovering above
With its pitch-dark attire
Then crashes heavily in torrent
Hurling hailstones
Exerts its existence with a terrifying thunderous laughter
and its fire of anger sparks across the sky as flashes of lightening
strikes down innocent lambs and children sheltered under the tree
Its heavy sighs snap and sweep away centuries of old banyans
Uproot and engulf tall towers and buildings
Shatter everything into sherds in seconds

And then it keeps quiet like an innocent infant
As if nothing has happened
Sleeping silently under the blue blanket
After being in spit and fury being obstinate
Or as if an unconcerned Rishi in deep meditation
When the air echoes with moaning and screaming.

I reflect on my childhood days
When it used to pour over the fields and forests
Naturally with drizzleor shower
To everyone`s pastimeand pleasure
Though sometimes with tumultuous tremor
Bearing a horrifying look
But in spite of it the farmers used to sing gleefully
One hand on the ploughhead
The other holding a stick
To break the idleness of the bullocks
And we children enjoyed rain and thunder equally
Without fear
Having no over enthusiastic reporters
Giving coverage for twenty-four hours
Or weather experts forecasting weather
And full-fledged preparation by government to avert a danger.

But nowadays it does not descend
In its regular cycle
As naturally as it used to
When I was a child…!
