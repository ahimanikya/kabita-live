Fallen Leaf

By Tamali Neogi

What is heroic in our heroes defeating evil by unfair means,
What is more important, action or contexts defining them?
The scholars argue, the socialists debate,
Aren`t the politicians the wisest beings?
Identities erased; look at the burning effigies
We blissfully forget what even a pigeon and a dove afford not,
Black, white or grey, in this grand show, Death equalizes all.
Here stands up the poet, the lead role in the parliament of ethics,
Ravana`s chariot is Rama`s vehicle, as Garuda of Vishnu,
Enemies are friends if the battles are won.
Arjuna or Karna, the game is lost, find out the holy grail,
Unfair it is that elixir was meant only for Gods.
Shadows of Kartikeya, the globe trotter, excitement ultimate,
Ganesha reaches the meaning in calm poise , the Fakir twits.
Attacked by the alien, the academicians stop a little,
Singing His praise! Let us crush under our feet, the fallen leaf, daring.

*Rama, Ravana and Arjuna , Karna are heroes and anti-heroes respectively from the great Indian epics Ramayana and Mahabharata. Kartikeya and Ganesha are the sons of Goddess Durga as per Hindu mythology. Kartikeya is renowned for his heroic attributes whereas Lord Ganesha is well known for his wisdom.
