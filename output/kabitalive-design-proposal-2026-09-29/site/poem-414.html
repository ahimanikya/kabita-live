That old woman

By Reema Sinha

I stole a quick glance at her,
She looked soft and somber.
Painful tears found their way,
down her weathered cheeks running away.

Suddenly a wide grin
appeared on her face,
She seemed reminiscing
that reduced her ache.

The moon showed off its full splendour,
In that light I could perfectly spot her.

She was preoccupied
thinking of her past,
she only had a scant attention
to me till the last.

There wasn`t any bonding,
there wasn`t any barrier
Don`t know why
my heart melted for her?

To me her emotions were beyond comprehension,
I could see, I could feel but couldn`t mention...
