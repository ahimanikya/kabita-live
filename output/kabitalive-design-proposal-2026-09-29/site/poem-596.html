THE SURVIVORS

By Sakti Sekhar Dash

Reader tools

It came and went away
Leaving behind, a land
Barely breathing,
And tired people.
We are a handful of living
In a dead world,
Shuffling about with heavy feet,
And stooping shoulders.
We are tired folks
With wills long dead and gone,
Facing the vast emptiness
Left by death and silence.
For a while, we rejoiced
That death spared us,
But with despair we realize
Death saved us for torment.
We dwell in a dead place,
Barely conversing,
Barely living
Flitting like furtive ghosts
Shuddering to confront
The silence and emptiness.
We would welcome
A change with glad hearts.
We are a few dying souls
In a dead world,
With wills, dead and gone,
Barely living, waiting for the end.
