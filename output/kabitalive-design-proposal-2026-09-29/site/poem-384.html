One more love

By Lisa Patnaik

She loved some one else
To see if that would
Make her to love you less.
Oh yes , it helped
To remind her poor soul
How much she loved you even more.
He said it was childhood
Long years and life goes on,
For her , time has never moved
From the moment she knew him.
The scar is still so fresh
As if she fell just a day ago
In love with his beautiful heart
Yet oblivious of state her`s was.
She tried not talking to your shadow
In stealth of nights , in solitude
Yet he happened to irk her soul
Cos he said he loved her no more.
Oh yes, it is for good
That he let the word out,
Now even the shadow is gone
Leaving behind unknown trails.
He says it was madness
Then she still suffers from it,
In awake world , she pretends to move on
In the other , she still speaks to his heart .
And yes, do not worry at all
She don`t ask for equal reaction for love,
Cos it no more needs him in it;
Love is now independent , floating feeling.
Thoughts now wander in search of him
Finding him no where near
They weave new poems about him
That is the best way to keep him around.
