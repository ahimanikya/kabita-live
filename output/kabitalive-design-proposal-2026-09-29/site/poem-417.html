THE VAPOURY SPREAD OF JATINGA

By Sangeeta Dey ( Roy)

When the sinking days of wintry twilights,
Metamorphose into a black curtain,
Jatinga is offered on her platter a vapoury spread.

Swirling fog hang heavy,
Lurking and beckoning you with thick lashes,
The cold chill touches you with a gentle caress,
It offers a feast to the eyes,
The eerie silence and white curls ,
Produces a mysterious sight before the iris.

The green canopy and the Blue hills reverberates a echo ,
As nature basks in exuberance.
The luscious thrill of foggy spells are a savoury spread with sketches of fairy tale sights.

The winding road takes you either uphill or downhill,
Brushing you with wet,slippery strokes
Against your skin,
Particularly your countenance.

The cloud capped hills
Are dressed with cottony fluffs,
And you can find yourself in the lap of cumulus.
White blankets dresses the peaks
And fly slowly.

When you talk of the hills calling,
Barialas the glorified picture is etched in our minds,
Yes,theBarial of Jatinga,
The Barial of DimaHasao,
Whose loving embrace you will cherish.

The serpentine road of Jatinga,
The blurry, virgin fog of Jatinga,
The yonder forest of Jatinga,
Which soaks you in the petals of enthralling serenity,
Erasing the mad rush of time count.

The mellifluous streams drenches you with its ecstatic sprinklers,
The swishing trees emanates whispers , you try to decipher,
Jatinga thus sits blissfully in the throne ,
Of one of the pages of Haflong`s story book,
Showering basketful of tranquility as you approach it`s door.
