Two poems

By Braja K Sorkar

< p> Journey

A narrow passage running through the deep forest of Time
Since my birth, crossing over the foot prints of ancient
civilizations created by the temporal settlers of earth.

It never ends,
I don`t know when it began.

The woods and living animals, wonderful creatures on
this vivid earth need nothing but
love and peace,
harmony of nature and men..

I have seen the unseen and
hidden treasures of Time
since my journey began through
the womb of night..

Time and again
I return alone!

(2) Self- indulgence

I needed it
Only because
you wanted to make this world
As your own ,
As you like it.

You deserve your own home.
I don`t have any idea
What makes a home!

I had nothing to offer you-
I am a nowhere man,
I belong to no man`s land!

Still
I needed it.

Only because
We had a dream ,
But did not learn how to make
A dream more memorable
For both of us..

Need of the hour
Is self- indulgence -
Once you said
Dear love….
