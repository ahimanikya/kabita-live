Every Loss is Fine*<

By Satyananda Sarangi

Reader tools
Saved places & passages

Every loss is fine if time`s long enough
To match its gain in coming years;
Every loss is fine if the path once rough
Finds its own smooth course amid fears.
Every loss is fine if its sorrow sparks
A change unattainable otherwise;
Every loss is fine if its indelible marks
Prove to be blessings in disguise.
Every loss is fine if it hasn`t a goal,
But still humbles the human soul.
