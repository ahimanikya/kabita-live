The design story

Ink & Voice

A colour that stays. A voice that flows. A page that holds them.

Kabita Live’s design begins with familiar things: a jamun stain, flowing water and the quiet of paper. Together, they make room for poetry in Odia, Hindi and English.

Familiar colours. A poetic connection.

ଜାମୁକୋଳିJamu Koli · Jamun stain
A memory that stays. Our mulberry accent recalls the lingering stain of jamun, like a line of poetry that stays with you.
ଝରଣା ଜଳJharana Jala · Spring water
A voice that flows. Aqua gives the water in our logo its life, carrying the idea of poetry moving from one person to another.
କାଗଜKagaja · Paper
A home for words. Soft white gives the page a gentle light, with space around the poem and dark ink for reading.

A small sign of flow.

Our leaf-and-stream symbol holds a simple thought: poetry flows from life.

Three languages, each with its own voice.

Newsreader brings warmth to our headings; Literata gives English poems a steady rhythm. Noto Serif Oriya and Tiro Devanagari Hindi honour the shapes of their scripts. Generous space lets each poem keep its own breath.
