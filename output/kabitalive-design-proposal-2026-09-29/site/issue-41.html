A small red insistence

One branch offers its colour to an otherwise quiet day. The flowers do not ask the whole landscape to change; they simply burn where they are. In their small red insistence lives a familiar courage: to keep a little warmth visible, even when the world is looking elsewhere.

Cultural connection

Palash provides the botanical and seasonal starting point; the emotional reading is original.
