I GRIEVE MY LOSS

By Sujata Dash

When one day bleeds into the next
like a mechanical robotic exercise
cold air wafts into life`s clumsy and fetid space
fessing up no palpable respite
dark clouds hover over
iridescent hues of vast expanse like a curse
my bright day headily metamorphoses to a bleak night

How quickly things change
at time`s behest!
that was balmy then and this is torrid of late
But why does this befall me all the time!
I am more or less clueless, believe me
the slither takes me away from seatherny
and I plummet like a doomed kite

My fine boned face framed by long flowing hair
seeks comforts in small wisps of air
to revel in sirimiri and nature`s misty clad oeuvre
croon a few aubade rhythms of jouissance
festooning my dawn in the susurrations of the beatific.
