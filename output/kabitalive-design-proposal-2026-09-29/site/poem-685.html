Needs of Gender Studies

By Biswanath Kundu

Reader tools
Saved places & passages

Select words in the poem to underline them. Saved on this device.

Mainstreaming of all as human resources with glee
Sans caste, race, sex and other biological identities
Or not being ever compartmentalized as he or she
Forms the basis of Gender Studies for human entities,
Where there will be no room for any gender biases
Nor will be there stereotyping on patriarchal routine
All attempts should originate from within, not on daises
Done, social justice n empowerment of all will get signal green.

Livings now in a global village connected are we with all
Thru` the clicking of mouse on computer sitting on drawing hall
On issues related to migration, development and human rights,
From lived experiences of all genders with troubles, travails n fights.

Gender Studies helps much to promote true picture
Of interdisciplinary and intersectional issues near n far
Inviting attention to injustice meted on gendered identity
Or effecting abolition of triple talaq system with gaiety
Or highlighting the rapists of Nirbhaya n Abhaya to the fore

And it allows no external judge to dictate one`s life paths anymore.
