Sangeeta Dey ( Roy)

Life and writing.

Sangeeta Dey (Roy) is a poet and essayist from Haflong in Assam’s Dima Hasao district. Her publisher biography describes her work in teaching and her co-editing of the poetry anthology ‘Mystic Reveries’. Her collections include ‘Whispers in the Blue’, published by Blue Rose in 2024, and ‘Twilight in the Woodland’, published by WissenMonk.

Landscape becomes an intimate presence in her Kabita Live poems. ‘The Vapoury Spread of Jatinga’ follows fog, winding roads and cloud-covered hills into a sense of welcome, while the poem presented under ‘Haflong’ turns midnight shadows into companions of inward reflection. ‘The Cycle’ questions the damage done by an inflated sense of self; ‘Sign Off’ considers the exhaustion concealed beneath outward warmth. These poems move between close observation of place and the emotional costs of human relationships.
