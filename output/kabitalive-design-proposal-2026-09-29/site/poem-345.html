Hello

By Aarti Mittal

It Is Me,
The Vacant Emptiness
Surrounded By Four
And Many More Walls
Accommodating
The Pleasantness Of Light
Fragrance
And
A Feel
Of Togetherness
With
The Echoes Of Giggles
From Variant Voices,
Sounds Of Moaning
And Mourning Of Yells In Silence,
Nevertheless
With
A Brimmed Exuberance

But....
Strangers
Or Maybe Known Uninvited
Enter Through
The Corners And Nooks,
Devouring The Walls
And Unclothing
Them Layer By Layer
Like A Patch Of Beauty,
Feeding On Emptiness
The Walls Stand
Dull And Bare
Building Up To Be Weaker
Dumb And Devoid

Stinking With The No Fragrance
Assuming A Sinister Voidness
