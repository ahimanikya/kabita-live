Illusion

By Gopa Nayak

Is it really an illusion
This life, the moments that I spend
In the warmth of hearth and heart?
It feels real, now and here
Who says its all up in the air?
I could taste it in the biryani
You had cooked for me
in your big grand kitchen
While the neem tree
peeped through the window
to watch over you, planting a kiss
on your sweet-heart`s nape.
I could feel in the many colours
That you loved watching me drape
Pink and yellow were the favorites
And then the perfumes, especially that one
While I waited for you at airport lounge at Amsterdam
A few euros though it had a strange smoky europeaness
Even I could bet no one can resist hugging the smell

And then all the music
that we loved and rarely listened
You always fancied singing to me
around friends and never could
Yet I knew your words made meanings
And when love wrote its notes
On the green leaves torn by storm
If this is all illusion
What then is life?

The taste the smell and that divine feeling
when you touch literally and otherwise
That is not illusion and if it is -
Let me die in that illusion
I don`t need that truth, naked and ugly
Cover me with the deceptive colour of pink
Which no one can ever define,
Which will be reflected only,
in the mirror of the deception.
Let me smell the multi-hued rose
When I wipe the golden statue ofKanha
And in the steaming hot Biryani at CP
Do touch me though even when I die
For I will hang onto that even in death
Illusion- Maya, my love, my soul mate.
