Sinister Sense

By Zahir Hossain Baidya

Heard our government has decided removal
Of the stray dogs from roads
So that rabies doesn`t spread often,
But oh what about those two-legged ones?
The two-legged ones who possess
Diseases as grim as that of rabies
In their filthy eyes,
In their distorted minds;
The two-legged ones who roam hither and thither
Without any shackles,
Usually in packs, sometimes singly,
On the sidewalks, on the station roads,
Or through the carriages of trains, on the buses.
Why don`t You poison their drinks?
Why don`t You explode the earbuds in their ears?
Why don`t You make them fall from trains?
Why don`t You paralyze their neck
Or burst its capillaries
When they turn it for a disgusting glance? —
So that our Floras can bloom
Without any worries of being stalked
Or sneaked up on or even hunted
in the cloaking shadows,
Without staying wide awake 24/7
For those street dogs and monstrous hyenas
Whilst walking alone.
I know it is neither a fairy-tale world nor a utopia
Here conflict is inevitable —
Be that as major as wars worldwide
Or as minor as frays within ourselves.
But oh Your mortals have become weak;
They have the eyes yet are blind,
They have the ears yet are deaf,
But verily You are the only Protector
Who can keep our Floras shield by Your will
Which is much needed
For it`s our Floras that make legends
With their motherly nurture,
With their loverly compassion.
