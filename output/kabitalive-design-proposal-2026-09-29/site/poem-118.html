The Caravan of Life

By MALAKSHMI BORTHAKUR

Reader tools

Why does this happen with us?
With all of us?
The stature of emotions often falls short in front of ego!
The words of the heart remain untold,
All the feelings either remain lost or
They are kept suppressed in the darkness of the heart.
The sole task the heart gets assigned is to beat only!
Sorrows are also covered with the false cloak of happiness!
And the tears are detained in the eyes only!
But sometimes, these naughty tears somehow spill out
With the spreading of kajal!

Still the caravan of life goes on and on and on
The cycle of life too goes along
We live, we live, we live...!
Likewise, on the wounds of the heart
The layers of ointment keep on increasingand increasing and increasing…!

Still,we wait for a handful of scattered drops of happiness!
And the wait goes on and on and on…!
