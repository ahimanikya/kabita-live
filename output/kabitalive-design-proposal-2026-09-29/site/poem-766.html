LOVE

By Akshaya Kumar Das

Reader tools
Saved places & passages

Love is a wonderful feeling,
Where the heart keeps reeling,

Love in childhood is Mother`s pride,
In adolescence beloved is the guide,

In youth the tiger hunts the tigress,
A soulful union is true bliss,

Creators magic spells,
Moods of the partner often compels,

Compels for a tight embrace,
To be lost within each other`s arms press,

Hold the beloved tight,
Never to leave her without a conjugal flight,

In hideous seeking a soulful union,
In deep embrace lost under the neon,

Love holds you mad in attraction,
Forget in each other`s arms for a breath-taking union,

Sighing like a furnace at the beloved,
In deep breath of palpitations lost & loved,

Behold by her beauty you turn a maniac,
A minute`s absence makes you sick,

Beseech her forever by your side,
Holding you tight in arms & closed eyes you hide,

Live, love & laugh is the mantra of life,
The planet holds in embrace as long as you live,
