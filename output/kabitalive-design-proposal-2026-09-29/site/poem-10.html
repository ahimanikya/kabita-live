RECLUSION

By Dilip Mohapatra

You are your own sacrificial goat
when you lead yourself to the altar
and wield your own scimitar to
chop off your head
again and again
for you have been taught that
sacrifice is the highest virtue
and that’s what the life is meant for.
You are constantly reminded of
the three fingers
that point at you
when you point one to the other
and you are told
that self flagellation
leads you
on the path to salvation.
It’s noble to be humble
and not to blow your trumpet
or spend time in front of the mirror
or even take a selfie
for that spells out your vanity
self love and self importance
reflects your narcissistic attitude
and you are reminded of
that you are just a speck in the infinite universe
or just a little tittle in the vast
epic verse…
You let people trample you
and use you as a doormat
but you just grin it and bear it
no protests of whimper
not a word in defence
and you attain
a kind of martyrdom....
But ultimately the time comes
when you let yourself
dissolve into
the miasma of anonymity
allow the black hole
to swallow you up
or you look for a safe
hiding place in your
very own shadow…
