Unfinished

By Maitreyee B Chowdhury

You dear poet,
you, with whom I walk empty streets
and Yet
we found no one to cry with, cry on-
You,
who never slept on broken park benches,
curled in hunger,
or anger
walked till the last drop of water dried in railway platforms-
you, who never screamed,
cried out loud,
wept for another-

Who, where, why and how, did we walk out of each other?

All around me,
this great restraint,
this white, harsh dreamlessness,
this immense propensity at indifference-

I begin and end with madness,
and like Poppy seeds fall asleep, deep-
without germination, water, or hope.

No song here that I haven`t heard ,
no truth
no love
no running wild
no madness
no searching eyes
no anger
no peace
no change
no solitude
no walks
no failures
as if a mountain had walked within us,
while you and I, we slept.

Unfinished, dear poet,
We
