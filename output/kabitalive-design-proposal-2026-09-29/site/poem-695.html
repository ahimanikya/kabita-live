Under the Umbrella of Your Love

By Namitarani Panda

Reader tools

Under the umbrella of your love
I bloom like a fragrant jasmine
Scorching rays turn to soothing sunshine
Turbulent torrents are gentle shower
And rocky paths are carpet of flowers
Under the umbrella of your love
Each step is an earnest promise
Of a new world full of pleasant surprises
Every sorrowful song is a melodious music
Infinite love, life, hope being its lyrics
Under the umbrella of your love
I learned what truly a shelter is
No walls, no roof yet a heavenly bliss
You by my side I stride as a river unafraid
As if an earth guarded in shifting shades
Under the umbrella of your love
Every path no matter how arduous and dark
I can walk through, you being my unfading spark
The world around me be full of thorns
But I dare to rise to a rose with your pious presence
With the wings of your deep love
I can map the sky like a golden dove
Under the umbrella of your love
Life is an Infinite fountain of happiness
As all my fear evaporates with a glance of your loving face
Your eyes full of assurances in my difficult days
Melt away all my sorrows and disappointments away
Under the umbrella of your love
Life is a soulful song
Supremely serene
A journey, despite ups and downs
Splendidly sublime
A mellifluous music from a flute
Truly divine
